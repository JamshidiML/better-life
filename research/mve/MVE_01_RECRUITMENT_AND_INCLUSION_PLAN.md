# MVE-01 Recruitment and Inclusion Plan

- Version: `0.2-draft`
- Status: Planning document only; do not recruit, advertise, screen, contact, or enroll

## Recruitment Objective

Recruit, only after approval, adults with varied perspectives who voluntarily identify a broad goal/context mismatch and can evaluate the low-fidelity concept. The objective is formative meaning, usability, safety, accessibility, and fit, not diagnosis prevalence, efficacy, or representativeness.

## Proposed Sample

Open hypothesis: 12-20 participants across two iterative rounds. Methods review must decide sample, rounds, stopping, comparator allocation, and whether the diversity objectives are feasible without tokenism or invalid subgroup inference.

## Candidate Inclusion Criteria

- Age 18 or older.
- Voluntarily identifies the broad research topic as potentially relevant; no explicit detail required.
- Can provide informed consent in an approved format/language with accommodations.
- Can choose a sufficiently private setting/contact method for the approved study.
- Understands the research is nonclinical, non-enforcing, and optional.

## Candidate Exclusion or Deferral Criteria

These are study-suitability boundaries, not diagnoses:

- Under 18.
- Immediate clinical/emergency help is being sought from the study.
- Participation appears coerced, third-party directed, or unsafe to keep private.
- Critical consent/boundary understanding cannot be established with approved accommodation.
- Research language/format cannot yet be made accessible.
- A conflict of interest or researcher/founder relationship would materially impair voluntariness or interpretation.

Clinical, ethics, coercive-control, accessibility, and privacy reviewers must approve the final criteria and response scripts. Do not collect a diagnosis or explicit reason to justify deferral.

## Inclusion Dimensions

Purposive coverage should consider, without claiming representation:

- gender and sexuality;
- faith, spiritual, secular, and culturally varied interpretations;
- German/English and later language needs;
- disability/accessibility, neurodiversity, literacy, and preferred communication format;
- age ranges within adults and varied digital confidence;
- prior/no experience with self-help, therapy, mutual aid, blocking tools, or paper planning;
- varied privacy contexts, including shared devices/households, only where participation can be safe;
- people who prefer no tool or reject recovery framing.

No dimension may be inferred from behavior. Collect only what is necessary for sampling/interpretation and approved by privacy/ethics review. A participant never represents a group.

## Prohibited Recruitment Practices

- No inferred-vulnerability targeting, intimate lookalike audiences, diagnosis advertising, fear, shame, urgency, or promised recovery.
- No recruitment from private support/fellowship spaces without organization/community authorization and ethics review.
- No partner, employer, clinician, family, ally, sponsor, school, religious group, or government pressure or access to participation/outcomes.
- No scraping, unsolicited sensitive direct messages, behavior monitoring, or proof of pornography/sexual behavior.
- No product access, care, reward, stronger protection, or payment contingent on enrollment.
- No founder's personal network as convenience recruitment without COI/voluntariness review.

## Draft Neutral Invitation Boundary

An eventual invitation may describe research on a private planning concept for adults who sometimes want help following a self-chosen goal. It must say: early research; not diagnosis/treatment/monitoring/blocking; no explicit detail needed; voluntary; risks/privacy limits; compensation terms; and how to decline/contact safely.

Exact copy and channels require all relevant review. No invitation is approved here.

## Screening Minimization

Proposed screening fields only: adult confirmation; broad relevance yes/no/unsure; approved language/format/accommodation feasibility; safe contact/session preference; conflict/research-role question; voluntary participation confirmation. Use `prefer not to answer` and do not ask diagnosis, frequency, content, URLs/apps, sexual history, abstinence, trauma, religion, relationship, treatment, or legal status.

Screening and contact data remain separate from research responses. Non-enrolled records receive an approved short retention/deletion rule.

## Compensation Principles

- Amount and mechanism remain open for ethics, equity, tax/legal, privacy, and founder decision.
- Compensation recognizes time/burden, not disclosure, completion, favorable feedback, outcome, risk, or reuse intent.
- Withdrawal/early stop does not forfeit compensation under the approved prorating/full-payment rule.
- Payment records are separate and use minimum identity data.
- Alternatives are needed for people excluded by a payment provider or bank access.

## Accessibility and Accommodation

Offer approved alternate formats, pacing, breaks, communication modes, assistive technology compatibility, support person only under a coercion/privacy-reviewed role, and quiet/private options. Ask what format helps without requiring diagnosis. Accommodation time is not failure or task burden.

## Safe Contact and Coercion Controls

- Participant selects a neutral contact channel/time and whether reminders are allowed.
- Messages use nonrevealing sender/subject/body approved by coercive-control/privacy review.
- No voicemail, SMS, calendar, mail, or follow-up by default.
- Do not ask an unsafe person for permission or send participation confirmation to another person.
- Provide discreet decline/reschedule/withdraw paths; do not attempt danger assessment or covert signaling without specialist-approved procedure.

## Recruitment Monitoring

If later executed, record aggregate funnel counts, channel, eligibility reason codes, accommodation availability, withdrawal, adverse events, and missing perspectives. Do not optimize conversion or conceal differential exclusion. Pause a channel when it creates coercion, stigma, privacy exposure, misleading expectations, or systematic inaccessibility.

## Authorization Gates

No recruitment until protocol, ethics, clinical, privacy/DPIA, coercive-control, accessibility/inclusion, method, data systems, compensation, current support routes, staff training, and founder authorization are documented and unexpired.
