# Better Life — Project Charter

Status: Founding charter v0.1  
Start date: 2026-07-10  
Founder and Product Owner: Mohsen Jamshidi

## 1. Mission

Build trustworthy technology that helps people protect their long-term values, health, relationships, work, and dignity during moments when emotion, compulsion, craving, stress, isolation, fatigue, or learned behavioral loops temporarily weaken self-control.

The first focus is unwanted compulsive pornography use and related digital sexual behavior in adults.

## 2. Founding Insight

A person can sincerely choose one course of action while calm and later act against that choice during a high-risk state. Better Life exists to help the calm-state person protect the high-risk-state person.

The product is therefore not merely a blocker, journal, streak counter, or AI chatbot. It is a voluntary self-governance system that combines:

- precommitment;
- environmental design;
- digital friction;
- behavioral support;
- privacy-preserving intelligence;
- optional human support;
- learning and recovery after lapses;
- gradual return of control to the user.

## 3. First Target Problem

Adults may repeatedly access pornography or engage in related digital sexual behavior despite personally unwanted consequences to work, relationships, sleep, finances, mental well-being, sexual functioning, values, or daily responsibilities.

The first product must help at the moment ordinary intention is not enough, while avoiding shame, coercion, overpathologizing, false clinical claims, and invasive surveillance.

## 4. First Target User — Working Hypothesis

An adult who:

- personally wants to reduce or stop an unwanted digital sexual behavior;
- has made repeated unsuccessful attempts using willpower, blockers, Screen Time, filters, accountability tools, therapy, coaching, groups, or self-help;
- experiences predictable high-risk windows, emotional states, environments, or device-use sequences;
- values stronger protection during those moments;
- voluntarily chooses the rules while calm;
- understands that no consumer system can honestly promise perfect prevention on every personally controlled device.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a final market definition.

## 5. User Promise

> Better Life helps you make a clear protection plan while calm, supports that plan when urges become strong, protects your privacy, and helps you recover and learn without shame when things do not go as intended.

## 6. Product Vision

A simple user experience supported by a layered technical system:

1. **Calm-state setup**
   - values and goals;
   - high-risk situations;
   - devices and access paths;
   - chosen protection strength;
   - allowed data;
   - support preferences;
   - exit and change rules.

2. **Protection layer**
   - website, app, search, feed, browser, device, and network friction where feasible;
   - time-, context-, and risk-sensitive controls;
   - transparent limits and no false claims.

3. **High-risk intervention layer**
   - pause and delay;
   - grounding;
   - environmental change;
   - replacement action;
   - self-authored reminders;
   - optional support contact.

4. **Learning and repair layer**
   - non-shaming debrief;
   - sequence mapping;
   - intervention effectiveness;
   - protection-plan improvement.

5. **Long-term autonomy layer**
   - strengthen skills and self-understanding;
   - reduce unnecessary restrictions when evidence supports it;
   - never optimize for permanent dependency on the product.

## 7. What Better Life Is Not

Better Life is not, at the current stage:

- a diagnosis;
- a therapist;
- a replacement for professional care;
- an emergency service;
- a moral judgment engine;
- a partner, parent, employer, school, religious-group, or government surveillance system;
- a hidden monitoring product;
- a guarantee that a user can never bypass protection;
- a system that stores or sells intimate behavioral data for advertising.

## 8. Non-Negotiable Safety Boundaries

- Adults only during the initial research and product phases.
- User-authored consent before strong controls.
- Consent must be specific, understandable, and auditable.
- No hidden monitoring.
- No raw explicit-content collection by default.
- No default screenshot sharing.
- No secret third-party configuration.
- No diagnosis or treatment claims without appropriate evidence and review.
- No shame-based engagement or public failure mechanics.
- No dark patterns that exploit fear, vulnerability, or relapse.
- No sale of sensitive personal data.
- Strong controls require a safe, pre-agreed calm-state modification and exit process.

## 9. Scientific Position

Better Life will distinguish among:

- high sexual desire;
- moral or religious incongruence;
- unwanted but non-clinical habits;
- problematic pornography use;
- compulsive sexual behavior with significant impairment;
- co-occurring mental-health, trauma, relationship, substance-use, or safety concerns.

The product must not assume that all frequent pornography use is addiction or disorder. It will use evidence-informed behavioral support while referring clinical assessment and treatment to qualified professionals.

## 10. Business Starting Point

Initial access:

- free core product during validation and early adoption;
- no advertising based on intimate data;
- no sale of behavioral profiles;
- no paywall that intentionally removes essential safety support during a crisis.

Longer-term working hypothesis:

- very low-cost membership, initially envisioned around EUR 1 per month;
- optional paid services only where they add real cost or professional value;
- possible grants, donations, public-health partnerships, employer-independent benefits, research funding, or NGO partnerships;
- final model must be tested for economic sustainability before commitment.

## 11. Success Metrics

We will not optimize primarily for screen time, daily streak obsession, fear, or user dependence.

Potential outcome metrics:

- reduction in personally unwanted behavior;
- reduction in severe high-risk episodes;
- improved perceived control and self-efficacy;
- fewer work, relationship, sleep, financial, or safety consequences;
- increased use of healthy replacement actions;
- improved recovery speed after a lapse;
- user trust and privacy comprehension;
- retention because the product is useful, not because it is addictive;
- successful tapering or reduced need for protection when appropriate.

All measures require validation and careful interpretation.

## 12. Strategic Questions To Resolve

1. What exact first user segment has the strongest need and highest willingness to adopt?
2. Which interventions are effective, acceptable, and safe in real high-risk moments?
3. What level of control do users voluntarily want?
4. Which platform should launch first?
5. What can be enforced honestly on iOS, Android, browsers, Windows, macOS, routers, and shared devices?
6. What data is truly necessary?
7. Which signals can stay on-device?
8. How should strict-mode changes and exits work without becoming either trivial to bypass or coercively permanent?
9. What role should clinicians, coaches, peer groups, allies, NGOs, and research institutions play?
10. What business model can keep the product accessible, trustworthy, independent, and sustainable?

## 13. Founding Decision Rule

When growth, technical power, revenue, or engagement conflicts with user autonomy, privacy, clinical honesty, or safety, the burden of proof is on the more powerful or more profitable option.

## 14. Definition Of A Successful First Product

The first product is successful when a real adult user can set up a meaningful protection plan in minutes, understand exactly what the system can and cannot do, receive useful support during a high-risk moment, keep intimate data protected, and feel more capable rather than more ashamed or controlled.
