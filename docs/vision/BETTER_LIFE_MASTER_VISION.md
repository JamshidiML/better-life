# Better Life — Master Vision

Status: Founder vision v0.1  
Date: 2026-07-15  
Purpose: Preserve the complete long-term vision without forcing every idea into the MVP.

## 1. Core Vision

Better Life is a living, intelligent recovery ecosystem that helps people protect themselves during moments when addiction, compulsion, craving, emotional dysregulation, isolation, stress, fatigue, or learned behavioral loops temporarily overpower their calm-state intentions.

The first focus is unwanted compulsive pornography use and related digital sexual behavior. The long-term system may extend to alcohol, drugs, gambling, compulsive gaming, social media, shopping, eating, and other harmful repetitive behaviors only after separate scientific, clinical, legal, and product validation.

## 2. Founding Inspiration

Better Life is inspired partly by the recovery principles developed through mutual-aid fellowships such as Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, and Sexaholics Anonymous:

- anonymity;
- shared experience;
- one person helping another;
- regular meetings;
- sponsorship and accountability;
- hope through lived experience;
- structured recovery practice;
- service to others;
- availability during moments of crisis.

Better Life should not impersonate, copy, replace, or claim affiliation with any fellowship without permission. Its goal is to learn from the human mechanisms that make mutual aid valuable and create a new, evidence-informed, privacy-first digital recovery network.

## 3. The Living Recovery Network

The long-term product should feel less like a static application and more like a living recovery organism.

It should combine:

- an always-available Guardian;
- a personalized Recovery Guide;
- a privacy-preserving human support network;
- virtual recovery meetings;
- personalized education and recovery plans;
- cross-device protection;
- early-risk prediction;
- environment-changing interventions;
- professional-care escalation when software is not enough;
- learning loops that continuously improve the user’s protection plan.

## 4. The Guardian Angel Concept

A working metaphor for the system is a “Guardian Angel”: a protective presence chosen by the user in a calm state and activated during vulnerable moments.

The Guardian Angel may:

- detect or receive a high-risk signal;
- delay access to a harmful path;
- block or redirect where technically feasible;
- simplify the screen and reduce stimulation;
- open a user-selected intervention;
- speak with the user;
- explain what is happening without shame;
- remind the user of their own reasons and commitments;
- guide breathing, grounding, urge surfing, movement, or environment change;
- suggest a replacement action;
- connect the user to safe human support;
- help the user leave a high-risk place;
- update the recovery plan after the event.

The metaphor must never imply supernatural certainty, clinical treatment, or infallibility.

## 5. The Protective Spiral

The founder’s “spiral” concept is preserved as a core product pattern.

When a user attempts a high-risk behavior, the system should remove the direct path and replace it with a sequence of protective steps. The purpose is not to trap or punish the user. The purpose is to create time, distance, reflection, regulation, and alternative action while the urge is strongest.

Possible sequence:

1. Detect or receive a high-risk action.
2. Apply immediate pause or block.
3. Reduce visual and digital stimulation.
4. Show a calm-state message written by the user.
5. Ask one simple state question.
6. Start a brief grounding or delay exercise.
7. Offer a meaningful replacement action.
8. Suggest changing physical environment.
9. Offer an anonymous peer-support request.
10. Offer a pre-approved trusted ally or professional support option.
11. Reassess risk.
12. Maintain, reduce, or safely release the intervention according to the user-authored policy.

The intervention must be progressively disclosed and simple during crisis. It must not overwhelm the user with many options.

## 6. Voluntary Strong Control

The founder wants the system to remain strong when a user in a high-risk state tries to remove protections.

Better Life should explore a consent-based strict mode in which the calm-state user pre-authorizes:

- delayed weakening of protection;
- a cooling-off period before uninstall or rule changes;
- multi-step confirmation;
- scheduled review windows;
- optional ally confirmation;
- platform-specific tamper friction;
- a protected recovery plan that cannot be changed instantly during a defined high-risk window.

Non-negotiable safeguards:

- adult voluntary opt-in;
- clear explanation before activation;
- no secret third-party setup;
- no permanent lock-in;
- emergency and safety exceptions;
- a documented calm-state exit process;
- coercion and abuse threat modeling;
- honest platform limitations;
- no claim of impossible-to-remove protection.

## 7. Anonymous Human Rescue Network

A major product concept is a 24/7 anonymous network that can connect a user in a high-risk moment with another safe participant.

The system may:

- send a minimal anonymous support request to eligible available volunteers;
- match by language, recovery domain, training, availability, and user preference;
- reveal no name, photograph, phone number, precise location, employer, or direct contact details;
- establish in-app voice, text, or audio communication through relays;
- use rotating pseudonymous identities;
- limit conversation duration and scope;
- provide an AI-supported conversation structure;
- offer escalation to a trained professional or crisis service when appropriate;
- collect minimal safety metadata without storing intimate conversation content by default.

### Anonymity Guard

The platform should explore real-time detection of accidental identity disclosure, including:

- full names;
- phone numbers;
- email addresses;
- precise addresses;
- social handles;
- employer or school names;
- precise meeting locations;
- direct-contact requests.

Possible interventions:

- warn before sending;
- redact or mute sensitive details;
- pause the conversation;
- require reconfirmation;
- end the session when risk is high;
- restrict future matching when abuse or repeated boundary violations occur.

Automatic permanent blocking of both people should not be the default response to an accidental disclosure. Safety actions must distinguish mistakes, manipulation, stalking, grooming, coercion, and repeated abuse.

### Relationship Continuity Without Identity Exposure

The platform may preserve continuity using:

- internal opaque user identifiers;
- pseudonymous relationship tokens;
- consented rematch preferences;
- reputation and trust signals;
- cryptographic or database-level identifiers inaccessible to users;
- moderated sponsor-like relationships with additional governance.

This must not create an unmonitored hidden social network.

## 8. AI-Facilitated Recovery Meetings

Better Life may host virtual meetings that are:

- anonymous;
- topic-specific;
- language-specific;
- recovery-domain-specific;
- moderated;
- accessible across time zones;
- personalized to stage of recovery and user needs.

AI may support meetings by:

- managing turn-taking;
- enforcing privacy and conduct rules;
- generating safe agendas;
- offering reflection prompts;
- identifying when professional moderation is required;
- summarizing only non-identifying group themes;
- translating between languages;
- detecting harassment, coercion, grooming, or identity disclosure;
- directing urgent safety issues away from peer support to appropriate services.

AI should not pretend to be a human sponsor, founder of a fellowship, clinician, or authority figure.

## 9. Personalized Recovery Program

Each user may receive a personalized plan built from:

- chosen goals;
- unwanted behaviors;
- values;
- triggers;
- high-risk times and environments;
- prior recovery attempts;
- device ecosystem;
- support preferences;
- treatment or group participation;
- self-reported emotional states;
- interventions that have previously helped;
- cultural and language preferences;
- accessibility needs.

Possible modules:

- education;
- values clarification;
- motivational work;
- implementation intentions;
- urge-management skills;
- emotion regulation;
- sleep and routine support;
- environmental restructuring;
- shame reduction and self-compassion;
- lapse review and repair;
- relationship and work-risk planning;
- referral to qualified care.

The product must distinguish self-help, peer support, coaching, psychotherapy, medical care, and emergency support.

## 10. Education System

Better Life may provide:

- short lessons;
- audio guidance;
- interactive exercises;
- recovery workbooks;
- personalized practice plans;
- reflection prompts;
- skills rehearsals;
- meeting preparation;
- relapse-prevention planning;
- non-clinical progress reviews.

Content must be evidence-reviewed, culturally adaptable, accessible, and free from moral humiliation.

## 11. Cross-Device Protection

The long-term vision includes coordinated protection across:

- mobile phones;
- tablets;
- browsers;
- laptops and desktops;
- routers and DNS;
- smart TVs;
- consoles;
- secondary devices;
- work or shared devices where legally and technically appropriate.

The product may combine:

- browser extensions;
- native apps;
- operating-system controls;
- local VPN or DNS components;
- desktop helpers;
- network guidance;
- account and policy synchronization;
- user-authored protection profiles.

No architecture may claim universal or impossible-to-bypass control. Borrowed devices, resets, alternate accounts, unmanaged devices, encrypted apps, local content, and physical access remain major limitations.

## 12. Risk Prediction And Context Awareness

The founder envisions prediction before a harmful action occurs.

Potential signals, only when justified and consented, include:

- time of day;
- high-risk schedule windows;
- device transitions;
- app or browser sequences;
- repeated attempts to weaken protection;
- self-reported mood or craving;
- sleep-related indicators;
- unusual isolation or unstructured time;
- calendar context;
- travel;
- optional coarse location categories;
- prior intervention outcomes.

Privacy principles:

- collect only what materially improves outcomes;
- prefer on-device processing;
- use coarse context instead of precise continuous location where possible;
- do not centralize raw location histories by default;
- allow per-signal consent;
- show what the model inferred and why;
- provide correction, deletion, and opt-out;
- evaluate false positives, false negatives, subgroup harms, and behavioral reactance.

## 13. Location And Environment

The founder wants the system to respond to high-risk locations, including work-related contexts where an impulsive sexual action could cause serious harm.

Better Life should evaluate lower-risk designs before continuous tracking:

- user-defined safe and high-risk zones stored on-device;
- coarse location categories;
- geofencing processed locally;
- time-and-place rules;
- manual work mode;
- calendar-triggered modes;
- Bluetooth or Wi-Fi context without storing exact location;
- user-confirmed context prompts.

Continuous precise location monitoring is a high-risk feature and requires strong necessity, proportionality, security, legal, and misuse justification.

## 14. Product Roles

Better Life may contain coordinated roles:

### Guardian

Protects, delays, blocks, redirects, and changes environment.

### Recovery Guide

Educates, plans, reflects, and personalizes non-clinical support.

### Companion

Provides calm, non-judgmental presence without pretending to be human or a therapist.

### Community Orchestrator

Connects anonymous human support, meetings, volunteers, and professionals safely.

### Safety Router

Recognizes when peer or software support is insufficient and directs users to qualified or emergency help.

### Privacy Guardian

Protects identity, limits data exposure, enforces consent, and detects unsafe disclosure.

## 15. Business And Access Vision

Initial access should be free.

Long-term hypotheses include:

- low-cost membership, potentially around EUR 1/month;
- grants;
- donations;
- social-enterprise funding;
- NGO partnerships;
- research partnerships;
- optional paid professional services;
- public-interest or health-system partnerships.

Core safety must not depend on advertising, sale of intimate data, or shame-driven upselling.

## 16. What Must Be Preserved

The following founder intentions are explicitly preserved:

- the project is much larger than a pornography blocker;
- mutual-aid fellowships are a central inspiration;
- the recovery network should become “living” and available at the moment of need;
- AI should coordinate prevention, education, support, and human connection;
- the system should create a protective spiral when a user seeks a harmful target;
- strong controls should resist impulsive removal when voluntarily chosen in advance;
- anonymity must be deeply protected;
- support should be available globally and around the clock;
- recovery plans and meetings should be personalized;
- the system should eventually operate across devices and environments;
- prediction and early intervention are long-term goals;
- the initial service should be free and later remain extremely accessible;
- new ideas will continue to be added without erasing prior vision.

## 17. Vision Versus Product Commitment

This document preserves the complete ambition. It does not approve every feature for implementation.

Each concept must pass:

1. User-value validation.
2. Scientific and clinical review.
3. Technical feasibility.
4. Privacy and security review.
5. Consent and coercion review.
6. Legal and regulatory assessment.
7. Misuse and abuse threat modeling.
8. Usability testing.
9. Operational sustainability analysis.

## 18. Long-Term North Star

Better Life should become a trusted recovery infrastructure that is present before, during, and after high-risk moments; combines human compassion with responsible technology; protects anonymity and dignity; and helps users become progressively more capable of living the life they deliberately choose.
