# Better Life

**Better Life** is a privacy-first behavioral technology startup building voluntary protection systems for adults who want help staying aligned with their long-term values during high-risk, emotionally dysregulated, or compulsive moments.

Our first product focus is unwanted compulsive pornography use and related digital sexual behavior. The long-term ambition is broader: help people strengthen self-governance when short-term urges conflict with health, relationships, work, dignity, and the life they deliberately choose.

## Product Thesis

People often make wise decisions in a calm state and reverse them in a high-risk state. Better Life turns calm-state intentions into transparent, user-authored protections that remain dependable when self-control is temporarily impaired.

The system should combine:

- **Voluntary precommitment:** the user defines the rules while calm.
- **Cross-device protection:** web, mobile, desktop, social platforms, and network layers where technically feasible.
- **High-risk-state support:** delay, friction, environmental change, grounding, replacement actions, and optional human support.
- **Privacy-first intelligence:** detect useful risk patterns with the minimum necessary data, preferably on-device.
- **Long-term autonomy:** protections should build capability rather than permanent dependence.

## Non-Negotiable Principle

**The user is the owner of the protection system.**

Better Life must not become partner spyware, employer monitoring, hidden surveillance, moral policing, or a tool for coercion. Strong controls are acceptable only when they are knowingly chosen by the adult user, clearly explained, auditable, and paired with a safe calm-state process for change, exit, and data deletion.

## Current Stage

**Phase 0 — Foundation, Discovery, Clinical Safety, and Technical Feasibility**

We are building the company and product foundation before committing to a final implementation. Current work includes:

1. Define the problem, first target user, and product doctrine.
2. Build an evidence map across psychology, behavioral science, clinical approaches, digital health, privacy, and platform capabilities.
3. Interview users, clinicians, coaches, researchers, and support organizations.
4. Validate the strongest and safest first product experience.
5. Test cross-device protection feasibility honestly on iOS, Android, browsers, desktop systems, and networks.
6. Build the first usable product only after its safety and value assumptions are explicit.

## First Product Direction

The first product is expected to be a simple, free, trust-first application experience with a shared web command center and platform-specific protection components where required. The final architecture is not yet fixed; it must be chosen from evidence and platform feasibility rather than preference.

Candidate components include:

- Calm-state protection plan / Device Constitution.
- High-risk windows and trigger planning.
- Manual and later privacy-preserving automated risk signals.
- Website and content friction where operating systems permit it.
- Social-media and browser protection layers.
- Urge-delay and environment-change interventions.
- Non-shaming lapse review and repair planning.
- Optional minimal-disclosure ally support.
- Professional-care signposting when software is not enough.

## Business Principle

The initial product should be free to reduce barriers to access and maximize learning. A later low-cost membership may support sustainability, but core safety and recovery capabilities must not be designed around exploiting vulnerability, shame, addictive engagement, or user-data monetization.

## Safety Position

Better Life is not currently a therapist, medical device, emergency service, or diagnostic system. It must not claim to diagnose, cure, treat, or guarantee prevention without the required evidence, professional review, and regulatory work.

## Working Rule

**Evidence before certainty. Consent before control. Privacy before prediction. Safety before growth. Autonomy as the final outcome.**
