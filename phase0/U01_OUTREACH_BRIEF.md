# U01 Outreach Brief

Issue: https://github.com/JamshidiML/p-vermeiden/issues/4

Objective: identify and screen the first Phase 0 user interview candidate without collecting explicit content or personal contact details in the repo.

## U01 Target Profile

U01 should ideally be:

- Adult, 18+.
- Participating voluntarily.
- iPhone-heavy or iOS-first.
- Has tried Apple Screen Time, a blocker, DNS filter, accountability app, or another self-control tool.
- Comfortable discussing patterns, devices, privacy, and support without explicit sexual details.
- Not currently in immediate safety crisis.

## Why This Profile First

An iOS-heavy first participant tests one of the hardest product realities early: platform limits. If the first discovery interview reveals that the user expects device-level control that iOS cannot honestly guarantee, we can adjust the Phase 1 MVP before building false promises.

Expected learning:

- What people think Screen Time or blockers should do.
- Where self-control tools feel supportive versus childish.
- Which bypasses are common on iPhone.
- Whether a Device Constitution feels respectful.
- Whether the user wants stronger friction, better self-reflection, ally support, or all three.

## Outreach Priority

Use private, low-pressure outreach in this order:

1. A trusted personal contact who has already spoken generally about digital habits or recovery.
2. A therapist, coach, or facilitator who can forward the invitation neutrally.
3. A private community where research recruitment is allowed.
4. A public-safe post only if replies can move to private messages.

Do not ask anyone to disclose interest publicly.

## Direct Message: English

```text
Hi. I am doing early product research for a privacy-first digital recovery tool for adults who want to reduce unwanted pornography use or related compulsive digital sexual behavior.

This is not therapy, diagnosis, or treatment. I am not asking for explicit sexual details, websites, screenshots, or browsing history. The interview is about patterns, devices, privacy, and what kind of support would feel safe and respectful.

I am especially looking to speak with someone who uses an iPhone and has tried Screen Time, blockers, or another self-control tool before.

Would you be open to a confidential 45-60 minute discovery interview? You can skip any question or stop at any time. If yes, I can send a short screener first.
```

## Referral Message For Therapist Or Coach

```text
I am doing Phase 0 product research for a privacy-first digital recovery tool for adults who voluntarily want to reduce unwanted pornography use or related compulsive digital sexual behavior.

This is product discovery only, not therapy, diagnosis, or treatment. Interviews avoid explicit sexual content, URLs, screenshots, and browsing history. The focus is on patterns, devices, privacy boundaries, support needs, and language that avoids shame.

For the first user interview, I am hoping to speak with an adult iPhone-heavy user who has tried Screen Time, blockers, or similar tools before.

If you know someone who might voluntarily want to participate, could you forward the invitation? Please do not share any client/patient details with me unless the person contacts me directly.
```

## Screener To Send After Interest

```text
Before scheduling, a few safety and fit questions:

1. Are you 18 or older?
2. Are you interested in reducing an unwanted digital sexual behavior, such as pornography use or related compulsive online behavior?
3. Are you choosing to participate voluntarily, without pressure from another person or organization?
4. Are you comfortable discussing patterns, emotions, devices, privacy, and support without sharing explicit sexual details?
5. Would this interview create any immediate safety concern for you?
6. Do you primarily use an iPhone or iPad? If not, which devices do you use most?
7. Have you tried Screen Time, blockers, DNS filters, accountability apps, therapy, coaching, groups, or no formal tools?
8. What words do you prefer: habit, compulsion, addiction, unwanted behavior, digital boundary, or something else?
9. Would you be willing to react to early product language such as a personal device agreement or non-shaming relapse debrief?
10. Do you prefer the interview in English, Persian, or another language?
```

## U01 Eligibility Decision

Mark U01 as eligible only if:

- Adult 18+.
- Voluntary participation confirmed.
- Comfortable with non-explicit discussion.
- No immediate safety concern.
- Willing to discuss device setup and prior tools.
- Contact details are stored outside the repo.

Do not proceed if:

- The person is under 18.
- Participation is pressured by someone else.
- The person wants therapy, diagnosis, emergency support, or crisis care.
- The person wants to share explicit content.
- A third party wants to answer on their behalf.

## First Interview Hypotheses

Before talking to U01, treat these as hypotheses, not truths:

1. iOS users may want stricter controls than Apple allows ordinary consumer apps to enforce.
2. Prior blockers may have failed because they block content too late in the relapse sequence.
3. Users may trust a Device Constitution more than partner-controlled accountability.
4. A non-shaming debrief may reveal earlier intervention points than a streak counter.
5. The first MVP may need to start as a recovery command center before device-level enforcement.

## What Success Looks Like

U01 outreach is successful when:

- One real candidate responds privately.
- Screener confirms eligibility.
- Interview is scheduled or ready to schedule.
- No explicit content or contact details are committed to the repo.
- Issue #4 is updated with non-identifying progress only.

## If No Candidate Responds

If no one responds after 48-72 hours:

- Try the therapist/coach referral message.
- Broaden from iOS-heavy to multi-device users.
- Use the public-safe version from `phase0/USER_RECRUITMENT_KIT.md`.
- Keep all replies private.
