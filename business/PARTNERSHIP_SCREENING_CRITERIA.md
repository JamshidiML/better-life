# Partnership screening criteria

Status: Cycle 1 Draft - no partnership or outreach authorized

## Pre-check

| Required element | Classification | Requirement before discussion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User problem | Evidence-supported conclusion | Named need that the partner can address better than internal work or a neutral link. |
| Expected benefit | Hypothesis | Specific learning, review, access, or operation; no halo/endorsement-only goal. |
| Supporting evidence | Design requirement | Current official scope plus independent references where risk warrants. |
| Required data | Design requirement | Data-flow inventory; default is no participant or intimate data. |
| Consent | Design requirement | User and participant choices are not bundled into the relationship. |
| Safety/misuse | Design requirement | Coercion, surveillance, shame, discrimination, clinical/crisis overreach, and commercial pressure analyzed. |
| Feasibility | Open question | Capability, staffing, jurisdiction, reliability, termination, and incident responsibilities verified. |
| Success metric | Hypothesis | Defined benefit, safety, privacy, independence, and cost measures. |
| Exit strategy | Design requirement | Termination, user continuity, data return/deletion, listing removal, and public statement process. |

## Mandatory gates

| Gate | Classification | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mission and independence | Design requirement | Partner cannot control doctrine, research result, user goal, care choice, or publication beyond legitimate confidentiality. |
| Safety and anti-coercion | Design requirement | No hidden monitoring, partner/employer control, shame, impossible-bypass, or emergency promise. |
| Evidence and claims | Design requirement | No unsupported clinical/outcome claim or transferred endorsement. |
| Privacy/security | Design requirement | Thread 07 review, minimal data, appropriate agreements, processors, incident/rights/deletion paths. |
| Inclusion | Design requirement | No discriminatory access, moral condemnation, forced theology, or exclusionary default. |
| Commercial fairness | Design requirement | Fees, referrals, ranking, conflicts, renewal/cancellation, and incentives are disclosed; no pay-to-rank support. |
| Operational accountability | Design requirement | Named owners, service limits, response/incident/appeal process, and audit right where needed. |
| Reputation/claim permission | Design requirement | Exact name/logo/quote/endorsement permissions in writing; default is no implied endorsement. |

## Red flags and automatic rejection

- **Design requirement:** demands raw participant notes, explicit content, browsing history, contact lists, or unrelated identifiers.
- **Design requirement:** proposes secret reporting to a partner, employer, parent, organization, insurer, advertiser, or seller.
- **Design requirement:** markets cure, guaranteed recovery, diagnosis from app data, or impossible-to-bypass control without valid evidence.
- **Design requirement:** refuses security/privacy diligence, conflict disclosure, user deletion/export rights, incident notification, or termination handling.
- **Design requirement:** uses shame, discrimination, conversion pressure, coercive religion/ideology, or vulnerable-user upselling.
- **Design requirement:** makes Better Life dependent on a proprietary provider without tested data/feature exit.
