## Advisory model

- **Verified:** No individual is a candidate merely because they publish, lead an organization, or appear in this research.
- **Hypothesis:** Role-first selection reduces prestige bias and makes missing perspectives visible before names are considered.
- **Verified:** Advisors challenge decisions; their involvement cannot be marketed as clinical validation or organizational endorsement without explicit scoped permission.

## Selection rubric (100 points)

| Criterion | Weight | Classification | Evidence sought |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Relevant competence and current practice | 20 | Verified | Credentials/work samples/references appropriate to role. |
| Safety, dignity, and non-coercion alignment | 20 | Verified | Prior work and scenario response. |
| Evidence/uncertainty discipline | 15 | Verified | Willingness to distinguish fact, hypothesis, and open question. |
| Privacy and confidentiality behavior | 10 | Verified | Data handling/confidentiality process. |
| Independence and conflict transparency | 10 | Verified | Written conflicts/funding/commercial interests. |
| Inclusive perspective | 10 | Hypothesis | Experience across relevant populations and non-pathologizing practice. |
| Constructive challenge and availability | 10 | Hypothesis | Scoped paid deliverable and response expectations. |
| Founder/team fit | 5 | Hypothesis | Clear communication without deference pressure. |

**Verified:** Automatic reject for coercive monitoring advocacy, unsupported cure/diagnosis claims, discriminatory/moralizing practice, confidentiality failure, concealed conflict, or request for raw participant notes.

## Governance

1. **Verified:** Written brief defines artifact, questions, decision rights, confidentiality, compensation, conflicts, and permission to name/quote.
2. **Verified:** Lived-experience advisors are compensated and may withdraw; one person is never presented as representative of a population.
3. **Verified:** The team records advice, evidence, disagreement, decision, owner, and date; advice is not silently adopted.
4. **Verified:** High-stakes claims require independent review from the relevant profession, not founder or advisor reputation alone.
5. **Open question:** Named shortlists and outreach begin only after founder approval of roles and budget.
