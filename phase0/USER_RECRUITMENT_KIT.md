# User Recruitment Kit

Issue: https://github.com/JamshidiML/p-vermeiden/issues/4

Purpose: recruit the first 5 adult user interview participants for Phase 0 discovery without collecting explicit sexual content, creating shame, or implying clinical treatment.

## Recruitment Goal

Recruit 5 adults who voluntarily want to reduce unwanted pornography use, compulsive masturbation, or related digital sexual behavior.

The first 5 interviews should maximize learning diversity, not statistical representativeness.

Aim for variation across:

- Device setup: iOS, Android, desktop-heavy, multi-device.
- Prior tools: blockers, accountability apps, therapy/coaching, no tools.
- Recovery framing: habit change, compulsion, addiction, faith-based, secular.
- Relationship context: single, partnered, married, private/self-led.
- Severity: mild disruption through high impairment, without recruiting people in acute crisis.

## Who To Recruit First

### Include

- Adults 18+.
- People participating voluntarily.
- People comfortable discussing patterns without explicit sexual details.
- People who have tried to change the behavior before, even informally.
- People willing to react to early product language such as Device Constitution, relapse debrief, and ally signals.

### Exclude For Phase 0

- Minors.
- Anyone participating because a partner, parent, employer, religious authority, or group is pressuring them.
- Anyone currently in immediate self-harm or harm-to-others crisis.
- Anyone who wants the interview to include explicit sexual details, URLs, images, or content descriptions.
- Anyone seeking therapy or emergency support from the product team.

## Recruitment Channels

Use channels where people already discuss behavior change, digital boundaries, recovery, productivity, faith/community support, or mental health. Avoid public posts that expose responders.

Potential channels:

- Personal network, one-to-one only.
- Therapists/coaches who can share a neutral research invitation, if appropriate.
- Recovery communities that allow research recruitment.
- Digital minimalism or habit-change communities.
- Anonymous interest form linked from a private message.
- University or founder network posts with neutral wording.

Avoid:

- Publicly tagging individuals.
- Asking people to disclose their issue in public comments.
- Posting in communities where recruitment is against rules.
- Promising a cure, blocker, diagnosis, or clinical help.

## Short Recruitment Message

Use this for direct messages or private community posts.

```text
I am doing early product research for a privacy-first digital recovery tool for adults who want to reduce unwanted pornography use or related compulsive digital sexual behavior.

This is not therapy, diagnosis, or treatment. The interview is about patterns, devices, privacy, and what kind of support would feel safe. You will not be asked to share explicit sexual details, websites, screenshots, or browsing history.

I am looking for 5 adults for a confidential 45-60 minute discovery interview. You can skip any question or stop at any time.

If you are interested, I can send a short screener first.
```

## Screener

Ask these before scheduling. Keep answers outside the public repo.

1. Are you 18 or older?
2. Are you interested in reducing an unwanted digital sexual behavior, such as pornography use or related compulsive online behavior?
3. Are you choosing to participate voluntarily, without pressure from another person or organization?
4. Are you comfortable discussing patterns, emotions, devices, privacy, and support without sharing explicit sexual details?
5. Would this interview create any immediate safety concern for you?
6. Which devices do you use most? iPhone, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, tablet, other.
7. Have you tried anything before? Blockers, DNS filters, accountability apps, therapy, coaching, groups, no tools, other.
8. What words do you prefer? Habit, compulsion, addiction, unwanted behavior, digital boundary, other.
9. Would you be willing to react to early product language such as a personal device agreement or non-shaming relapse debrief?
10. Do you prefer interview language: English, Persian, or other?

## Interviewer Checklist

Before interview:

- Assign participant code, such as `U01`.
- Store contact details outside the repo.
- Confirm whether recording is off or separately consented.
- Open `phase0/INTERVIEW_GUIDES.md`.
- Open `phase0/INTERVIEW_NOTE_TEMPLATE.md`.
- Re-read `phase0/SAFETY_AND_CONSENT.md`.

During interview:

- Read consent script.
- Confirm age 18+.
- Confirm voluntary participation.
- Redirect away from explicit content.
- Capture sequences, not content.
- Watch for distress or coercion.

After interview:

- Save notes using participant code only.
- Remove identifying details.
- Update candidate pipeline status.
- Add risk-register notes if needed.
- Do not paste raw notes into external AI tools.

## First 5 Participant Mix

Try to recruit:

1. iPhone-heavy user who has tried Screen Time or blockers.
2. Android-heavy user who has tried app blockers or VPN/DNS tools.
3. Desktop-heavy user who uses multiple browsers or work devices.
4. User with prior therapy, coaching, or recovery-group experience.
5. User who has not used any formal tool yet.

This mix should reveal platform constraints, trust barriers, and language differences early.

## Success Criteria For Issue #4

Issue #4 is complete when:

- 5 candidates are identified.
- Each candidate passes the screener.
- Each candidate is 18+.
- Each candidate confirms voluntary participation.
- No candidate is asked for explicit content.
- Interviews are scheduled or ready to schedule.
- Candidate records use participant codes only.

## Risks And Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Participant feels exposed or ashamed | Use neutral language, private outreach, skip/stop rights |
| Public comments reveal sensitive interest | Ask interested people to message privately; do not ask for public disclosure |
| Coerced participation | Ask voluntary-participation screener question; stop if coercion appears |
| Interview drifts into therapy | Repeat research-not-therapy boundary; stop if needed |
| Explicit content is shared | Redirect once; stop if it continues |
| Raw notes leak | Use participant codes; separate contact details; no raw notes in AI tools |
