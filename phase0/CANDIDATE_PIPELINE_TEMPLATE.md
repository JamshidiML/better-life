## Status Values

Screener status:

- Not screened
- Eligible
- Not eligible
- Needs safety follow-up
- Declined

Interview status:

- Not scheduled
- Scheduled
- Completed
- No-show
- Cancelled
- Paused for safety

## Storage Rules

- Contact details live outside this repo.
- Interview notes use `phase0/INTERVIEW_NOTE_TEMPLATE.md`.
- Notes should be named by participant code only.
- Do not store explicit content.
- Do not store raw URLs, screenshots, or browsing history.
- Do not paste raw notes into external AI tools.
