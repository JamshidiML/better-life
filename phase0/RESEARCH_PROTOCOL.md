# Phase 0 Research Protocol

This protocol is for discovery research, not clinical treatment or diagnosis. Participants should understand that the research is about product design for voluntary behavior change and digital self-regulation.

## Research Questions

1. What are the most common sequences that precede unwanted pornography use or compulsive masturbation?
2. Which moments in the sequence are early enough for useful intervention?
3. Which interventions feel supportive, and which feel shaming or controlling?
4. What are users willing to let software observe, store, infer, or share?
5. What data should stay local by default?
6. What should an ally see, if anything?
7. What bypasses do users actually use, and what causes them?
8. Which device setups are most common?
9. Which users need strong friction versus light coaching?
10. What language should the product avoid?
11. When should the product tell the user to seek professional support?
12. What is safe enough for Phase 1?

## Participant Segments

### Adult Users

Recruit adults who self-identify as wanting to reduce unwanted pornography use, compulsive masturbation, or related digital sexual behavior. Do not require them to accept the word "addiction."

Aim for variation:

- Severity: mild, moderate, high impairment.
- Recovery model: secular, religious, therapy-led, group-led, self-led.
- Relationship context: single, dating, married/partnered, separated.
- Device setup: iPhone-only, Android-only, desktop-heavy, multi-device, technical/power user.
- Past tools: blocker, DNS filter, accountability app, therapy, coaching, groups, no tools.

### Therapists, Coaches, And Group Facilitators

Recruit people who have professional or structured experience supporting compulsive sexual behavior, behavioral addictions, digital habits, pornography concerns, or adjacent mental health issues.

Topics:

- Safety language.
- Boundaries between software and treatment.
- Relapse repair.
- Shame reduction.
- Partner/family dynamics.
- When software should escalate or get out of the way.

### Allies, Partners, And Supporters

Recruit adults who have supported someone trying to change unwanted pornography use or compulsive digital behavior.

Topics:

- What information helps.
- What information harms.
- Burnout.
- Coercion risk.
- Partner-safe accountability.
- Minimal-disclosure alerts.

## Exclusion And Safety Rules

- Do not interview minors in Phase 0 until legal, guardian-consent, and child-safety protocols exist.
- Do not ask for explicit sexual details, explicit URLs, images, filenames, or content descriptions.
- If a participant reports imminent risk of self-harm or harm to another person, stop research mode and follow the safety escalation protocol.
- If a participant appears coerced by a partner, parent, employer, or religious authority, do not proceed with accountability-product validation. Move to safety-oriented questions only.
- If the interview becomes distressing, pause, offer to skip, or end the session.

## Recruitment Screener

Use plain language. Do not ask for explicit content.

1. Are you 18 or older?
2. Are you interested in reducing a digital sexual behavior that feels unwanted or hard to control?
3. Which devices do you use most often? Phone, laptop, desktop, tablet, other.
4. Which operating systems do you use? iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, other.
5. Have you tried any tools before? Blockers, DNS filters, accountability apps, therapy, coaching, groups, none.
6. Are you comfortable talking about patterns and technology without sharing explicit sexual details?
7. Would discussing this topic today create a safety concern for you?
8. Are you participating voluntarily, without pressure from another person?

## Consent Checklist

Before each interview, confirm:

- The participant can stop at any time.
- They do not need to answer any question.
- The session is for product research, not therapy.
- The interviewer will not ask for explicit content.
- Notes will avoid explicit details and unnecessary identifiers.
- Any recording requires separate consent.
- The participant can request deletion of their notes.
- If safety risk appears, the interviewer may pause research and direct them to immediate support.

## Interview Workflow

1. Consent and safety check, 5 minutes.
2. Context and goals, 10 minutes.
3. Behavior sequence mapping, 15 minutes.
4. Device and bypass mapping, 10 minutes.
5. Reactions to prototype language, 15 minutes.
6. Closing reflection and deletion preference, 5 minutes.

## Data Handling

Use data minimization from the beginning.

- Do not store explicit sexual content.
- Do not store raw browsing history.
- Do not store names in synthesis docs.
- Use participant codes such as U01, T03, A02.
- Separate contact information from notes.
- Mark quotes as sensitive.
- Delete raw recordings after notes are checked, unless there is a documented reason to keep them.
- Keep a deletion request log.

## Synthesis Method

After every 5 interviews:

- Extract relapse sequence patterns.
- Extract intervention moments.
- Extract trust breakers.
- Extract language that works.
- Extract language that fails.
- Extract feature red flags.
- Update the risk register.
- Update Phase 1 assumptions.

## Evidence Standard For Phase 1

A feature can move into Phase 1 only if:

- It solves a repeated user problem.
- It does not require collecting explicit content.
- It has an understandable consent model.
- It has a safety fallback.
- It does not depend on impossible platform guarantees.
- It does not create obvious coercion or surveillance risk.
