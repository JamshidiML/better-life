# MVE-01 Participant Journey

- Version: `0.2-draft`
- Status: Journey for specialist/ethics review only; no participant execution

## Journey Invariants

- Participation is voluntary, adult-only, nonclinical, and independent of product access, care, payment, reward, ally, employer, family, or fellowship.
- `Stop`, `skip`, `prefer not to answer`, `use broad labels`, and `withdraw` remain available throughout.
- No explicit sexual/content detail, diagnosis, trauma narrative, third-party identity, browsing history, or proof of behavior is requested.
- Compensation, if later approved, is not conditioned on completion or favorable feedback.
- The facilitator does not assess calmness/capacity, diagnose, counsel, act as a sponsor/peer, or promise emergency response.

## Participant-Facing Boundary Draft

> This is an early research activity about a possible private planning tool. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor, or block anything. You can use broad words, skip any question, stop, or withdraw without giving a reason. Please do not share explicit details or another person's identifying information.

This copy is draft and requires clinical, privacy, coercive-control, accessibility, language, and ethics review.

## Critical Task Definition

Critical tasks are: understand voluntary/nonclinical scope; distinguish prototype from research records; stop/withdraw; choose skip/prefer-not-to-answer; close/discard; understand local/use-once choice; understand deletion limits; reach an independently reviewed other-support route.

Required accommodations are part of the task, not facilitator correction. A critical task cannot be counted successful if the facilitator chooses or performs it for the participant.

## Exceptional Paths

- `Privacy concern`: stop screen sharing/recording, close materials, follow approved discreet-contact and incident procedure.
- `Distress or shame`: pause, offer stop, avoid probing, use approved adverse-event procedure and neutral support routes.
- `Coercion concern`: do not contact another person or ask for proof; offer discreet stop/reschedule and follow the specialist-approved plan.
- `Immediate help request`: state researcher limits and provide independently reviewed current routes; no enrollment pressure.
- `Explicit disclosure`: interrupt respectfully, explain minimization, do not record the detail, and assess only study continuation under approved rules.

## Completion and Exit

A session is complete when the participant has left the research interaction safely, not when all tasks are finished. Withdrawal, early stop, and missing data are valid outcomes and remain in the disclosed analysis denominator.
