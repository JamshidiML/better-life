## Convergent findings

### Product foundation

1. **Hypothesis:** The lowest-risk first value test is a one-page personal plan plus a manual Protective Spiral using static reviewed content.
2. **Verified:** The first journey does not require an account, payment, AI, monitoring, explicit content, ally, or peer.
3. **Verified:** The broad long-term vision does not justify a layered suite before the small core proves value.
4. **Verified:** If the manual/local core lacks incremental value, the correct response is to stop or reframe, not add surveillance or engagement complexity.

### Scientific and clinical foundation

1. **Verified:** WHO CSBD terminology may anchor research context but the product cannot diagnose a user.
2. **Evidence-supported:** PPU/CSBD intervention research offers candidate mechanisms with substantial transfer, bias, heterogeneity, and population limitations.
3. **Verified:** High frequency, high desire, moral incongruence, distress, impaired control, and functional impact must not be collapsed into one app label.
4. **Verified:** No Better Life treatment, cure, prevention, clinical-proof, or emergency claim is approved.

### Privacy and consent foundation

1. **Verified:** Raw explicit content, screenshots, raw browsing histories, secret partner reports, sensitive inferences, ad use, and model training on intimate data are prohibited.
2. **Hypothesis:** Accountless/local storage can reduce server, processor, recovery, and coercion exposure for the first shape.
3. **Verified:** Local does not mean risk-free; shared devices, OS backup, notifications, export, malware, and coercive control remain.
4. **Verified:** Consent is capability-specific and cannot make an unsafe design acceptable.

### Architecture foundation

1. **Platform limitation:** No browser, mobile, desktop, DNS, or network layer provides universal motivated-adult enforcement.
2. **Hypothesis:** A Chromium adapter is the fastest bounded enforcement experiment after manual-core value and privacy review.
3. **Hypothesis:** Android `VpnService` is the next enforcement spike; iOS work depends on entitlement and distribution outcomes.
4. **Verified:** Every enforcement statement must include bypass, false-block, legitimate-use recovery, and exit behavior.

### Human support and ecosystem foundation

1. **Verified:** Peers and AI cannot be therapists, sponsors, moderators of their own conflicts, or emergency responders.
2. **Verified:** Better Life must not copy fellowship identity or imply affiliation/endorsement.
3. **Verified:** Live peer support has critical safety, privacy, moderation, incident, and economic dependencies and remains deferred.
4. **Hypothesis:** Early external work should buy independent challenge from clinical, privacy, security, coercive-control, accessibility, method, and lived-experience perspectives.

### Business foundation

1. **Verified:** EUR1 monthly card billing has severe fixed-fee drag; annual/prepaid collection improves contribution but changes accessibility and consumer obligations.
2. **Verified:** Free safety, consent, deletion/export, care routes, and security fixes cannot be paywalled.
3. **Verified:** Advertising, intimate-data monetization, shame upsells, vulnerable-user pricing, paid care ranking, and secret organization reporting are rejected.
4. **Open question:** Legal entity, tax/VAT, insurance, security/privacy reserve, support burden, conversion, churn, and willingness to pay are unresolved.
