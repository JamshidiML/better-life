# Phase 0 Interview Guides

These guides are semi-structured. Use them to learn patterns, not to force every participant through every question.

## Shared Interview Rules

- Do not ask for explicit sexual content.
- Ask about sequences, emotions, devices, timing, and support.
- Let participants skip any question.
- Avoid terms like "addict" unless the participant uses them first.
- Do not promise that software can fully prevent relapse.
- End with grounding and a neutral next step.

## User Interview Guide

### Opening

1. What made this problem important enough for you to talk about it?
2. What words do you prefer for the behavior: habit, compulsion, addiction, unwanted behavior, something else?
3. What would meaningful progress look like in 3 months?

### Current Pattern

4. Without sharing explicit details, what usually happens before an unwanted session?
5. What time of day is most risky?
6. What emotion or situation most often appears beforehand?
7. What is the earliest signal that the pattern has started?
8. What happens after a lapse that makes the next day better or worse?

### Device And Environment

9. Which device is most often involved?
10. Which apps, browsers, or device states are usually part of the sequence?
11. Where are you physically when it happens?
12. What role do sleep, stress, loneliness, boredom, or conflict play?
13. Are there secondary devices or old devices that matter?

### Prior Tools

14. What have you tried before?
15. What worked even a little?
16. What failed?
17. What did you bypass, and why?
18. What made a tool feel supportive?
19. What made a tool feel insulting, childish, or unsafe?

### Consent And Privacy

20. What would you allow an app to observe locally?
21. What would you never want stored?
22. What could be shared with a trusted person?
23. What should never be shared with a trusted person?
24. Would you accept delayed override during high-risk windows?
25. Would you accept stronger controls that you set up while calm?

### Prototype Reactions

Read or show short versions of:

- Device Constitution.
- Relapse debrief.
- Ally signal.
- Urge Delay Exchange.

Questions:

26. What feels respectful?
27. What feels controlling?
28. What words would you change?
29. Which prototype would have helped in a real moment?
30. Which prototype would you ignore or bypass?

### Closing

31. What should software never do in this area?
32. What would make you trust this product?
33. What would make you uninstall it?
34. Is there anything we missed that matters?

## Therapist, Coach, Or Facilitator Interview Guide

### Practice Context

1. What kinds of clients or participants do you support around unwanted sexual behavior or digital compulsions?
2. What language do you use, and what language do you avoid?
3. What do people misunderstand about this problem?

### Clinical And Support Boundaries

4. Where can software help?
5. Where should software not intervene?
6. What signs suggest the user should seek professional care?
7. What signs suggest crisis or immediate safety risk?
8. How should software respond after a lapse?

### Relapse Patterns

9. What sequences do you commonly see before relapse?
10. Which early signals are most useful?
11. Which interventions tend to work in the moment?
12. Which interventions backfire?

### Shame, Identity, And Repair

13. What makes shame worse?
14. What helps people repair after a lapse?
15. How should a product talk about streaks, progress, and setbacks?
16. How should it handle religious or moral frameworks without increasing harm?

### Accountability

17. What should allies see?
18. What should allies not see?
19. What are the risks in couples or family systems?
20. How can software prevent coercive monitoring?

### Prototype Review

21. Review Device Constitution. What is missing?
22. Review relapse debrief. What is unsafe or unhelpful?
23. Review ally signal copy. What could harm relationships?
24. What would you require before recommending this to someone?

## Ally, Partner, Or Supporter Interview Guide

### Context

1. What kind of support have you provided?
2. What felt helpful?
3. What felt heavy, confusing, or unsafe?

### Information Boundaries

4. What information would help you support someone?
5. What information would be too much?
6. Would a green/yellow/red signal be useful?
7. What would you do if you received a red signal?
8. What would help you avoid becoming the person's police officer?

### Relationship Safety

9. How could accountability damage trust?
10. What should remain private even inside a relationship?
11. What consent should be renewed?
12. What should happen if the person wants to leave the accountability setup?

### Prototype Review

13. Review ally signal copy. What would you change?
14. Review weekly truth session. Would you use this?
15. Review emergency hand-raise. What response would feel realistic?

### Closing

16. What advice would you give the product team?
17. What failure mode worries you most?
