# Device Constitution Draft

The Device Constitution is a user-authored agreement written while calm. It defines what the user wants, what help is allowed, what data may be used, and how controls can be changed.

This is a prototype for interviews, not final product copy.

## Short Version

I am choosing these boundaries to protect the life I want. During high-risk moments, I want my devices to follow the rules I wrote while calm. I can change these rules during calm review windows, not during an urge.

## User Goals

I am using this because I want:

- More control over my attention.
- Less unwanted pornography use.
- Less compulsive sexual behavior.
- Better sleep.
- Better relationships.
- More honesty with myself.
- More time for work, study, health, faith, creativity, or family.

## My High-Risk Windows

I want extra protection during:

- Late night.
- When I am alone.
- After conflict.
- After stress.
- When I am sleep deprived.
- During travel.
- During unstructured time.
- Other: [user writes]

## My Protected Settings

During high-risk windows, I do not want to be able to quickly:

- Disable the browser extension.
- Disable local VPN or DNS protection.
- Change DNS settings.
- Install a new browser.
- Use private browsing without friction.
- Install known bypass tools.
- Remove the recovery app.
- Change ally-sharing settings.

## My Override Rules

If I want to override a protection, I choose:

- Wait 2 minutes.
- Wait 10 minutes.
- Wait until tomorrow.
- Complete a replacement action first.
- Ask an ally for approval.
- Use a dual-key override.
- Allow override only during a scheduled calm review window.

## My Ally Sharing

My ally may see:

- Green/yellow/red support level.
- Help requests I send.
- Tamper attempts if I choose.
- Weekly review summary if I approve.

My ally may not see by default:

- Raw browsing history.
- Explicit content details.
- Screenshots.
- Private notes.
- Search terms.
- My fantasies or sexual details.

## My Exit Process

I can leave the system through:

- A 24-hour calm-state waiting period.
- A scheduled review.
- Exporting my data.
- Deleting sensitive notes.
- Notifying allies only if I previously chose that.

## Tapering

I want controls to reduce when:

- I have stable sleep.
- I have fewer high-risk loops.
- I repair lapses quickly.
- I stop trying to bypass controls.
- I keep commitments across devices.
- I choose to review calmly.

## Interview Questions

1. What feels respectful in this constitution?
2. What feels too controlling?
3. What is missing?
4. What would you never agree to?
5. What should be impossible to change during a craving?
6. What should always remain changeable?
