# MVE-01 Analysis Plan

- Version: `0.3-draft`
- Status: Draft for methods/ethics review; no data exist and no analysis was performed
- Claim ceiling: Formative description of the exact studied version, sample, languages, modalities, and conditions

## Analysis Questions

1. Is the problem framing relevant and nonclinical/nonmoralizing?
2. Are five decisions and product/data boundaries understood?
3. Can critical tasks, including exit/discard, be completed with accommodations and without facilitator correction?
4. Is the manual Spiral experienced as useful and agency-supporting?
5. What burden, shame, reactance, rumination, distress, privacy regret, accessibility, cultural-fit, or care-displacement harms occur?
6. How do participants compare MVE-01 with an approved paper/no-tool condition?
7. What evidence supports revise, simplify, stop, or consider a later local Supportive prototype?

## Preregistration and Version Lock

Before enrollment, register the approved protocol, materials, condition/order, measures, thresholds, sample/round logic, exclusions, analysis codebook, missingness, adverse-event, and deviation rules in the approved system. No public registration location is assumed.

Materially changed MVE versions receive separate IDs and analyses. Do not pool across rounds unless methods review predefines which unchanged measures/tasks are comparable.

## Analysis Populations

Report a transparent flow:

- approached only if ethically reportable in aggregate;
- screened;
- eligible/ineligible/deferred by coded reason;
- consented;
- session started;
- each condition/task exposed;
- withdrawn/stopped;
- completed;
- included in each measure denominator.

Primary formative denominator proposal: every participant who starts the relevant task/condition. Withdrawals, skips, adverse stops, and missing responses remain visible and are never silently converted to success or excluded from safety interpretation.

## Quantitative Description

- Binary/item tasks: exact count and denominator; participant-level pattern; facilitator correction and accommodation reported separately.
- Ratings: full distribution, count, median, range, missing/prefer-not-to-answer; avoid precision unsupported by sample.
- Time: median/range or IQR if justified; separate active time, breaks, accessibility setup, facilitator clarification, and interruption.
- Preference: exact choices including `none` and `cannot compare`, with condition order.
- Adverse events: individual event domain/level/disposition in protected review plus nonidentifying aggregate summary.

Confidence intervals or percentages may be shown only if methods review finds them useful and their small-sample limitation is explicit. No null-hypothesis significance testing, effect-size efficacy claim, prevalence estimate, or population ranking is planned.

## Decision Rule Categories

### Category A - Zero-Tolerance Individual Failures

Analyze inability to stop/exit/withdraw, coercive participation, material privacy disclosure, inaccessible critical route, care displacement, severe attributable harm, facilitator clinical-role breach, and unauthorized recording/contact as individual cases. These are not statistical thresholds. One credible event may pause or stop the relevant procedure or study and requires independent adjudication, minimum-necessary incident handling, and a documented disposition. Never pool, average, discount, or exclude the case because other participants had favorable outcomes.

### Category B - Formative Usability Signals

Describe comprehension, task completion, time, facilitator correction, burden, privacy mental model, usefulness, agency, and noncritical accessibility friction by exact version and condition. Counts, percentages, medians, and ranges guide iteration only. In a small formative sample they do not validate the instrument, establish safety/usability, or estimate population performance.

### Category C - Descriptive Research Outcomes

Describe preference, willingness to reuse, ratings, qualitative themes, cultural fit, and paper/no-tool comparison with exact context, negative cases, order, and missingness. These outcomes cannot authorize progression alone. They may support revision, retirement, or a proposal for separately reviewed later research.

## Comparator Analysis

Comparator is unresolved. If approved:

- State whether within-participant or between-condition and justify order/counterbalancing.
- Compare task interpretation, usefulness, agency, burden, shame/reactance, privacy, preference, time, and adverse effects without calling differences treatment effects.
- Report carryover, novelty, facilitator, and unequal-information limitations.
- `No tool/current coping` is not assumed to be inert, standardized, or inferior.
- Paper may create privacy/accessibility burdens and requires its own exit/disposal controls.

## Qualitative Analysis

Proposed structured framework domains:

- relevance/need and safer alternatives;
- clinical/moral/ideological interpretation;
- five-decision meaning and missing options;
- Spiral usefulness/agency and mechanism interpretation;
- privacy/storage/deletion mental model;
- burden/shame/reactance/rumination/distress/care displacement;
- accessibility, language, culture, faith/secular, sexuality/gender, literacy, prior-experience fit;
- exit/other-support and reuse conditions;
- preference and stop-the-concept evidence.

Methods review must decide coding approach, number/independence of coders, reflexivity, disagreement, audit trail, quote use, negative-case analysis, and whether recordings are necessary. Preserve contradictory and disconfirming cases; do not turn themes into prevalence.

## Mixed Decision Synthesis

Use a joint decision table by research question:

| Category | Evidence use | Requires revision | Pause/stop authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A - individual failure | Case record and independent adjudication; no aggregate pass | Exact affected procedure/system/material must be corrected before any proposed restart | One credible event may pause/stop; favorable B/C evidence cannot override |
| B - usability | Version-specific iteration using counts, cases, corrections, accommodations, distributions and timing | Confusion, correction, burden, privacy-model, usefulness, agency or access pattern | Does not independently prove safety or authorize progression; unresolved pattern may justify conservative pause/revision |
| C - descriptive | Contextualize preference, reuse, ratings, themes, cultural fit and comparator observations | Negative/contradictory cases, poor fit or safer preferred alternative | Cannot authorize progression alone; may support concept retirement or block a claim |

Qualitative contradiction can block a claim or next-step recommendation even when a numeric observation appears favorable. No Category B percentage or Category C result is a `pass`. A recommendation may only ask the authorized owners to consider a later reviewed local Supportive prototype; it does not authorize MVP, efficacy, production, enforcement, AI, social, reward, or payment scope.

## Missingness and Withdrawal

Report reason only when voluntarily provided and safe. Do not impute favorable outcomes. Analyze whether missingness/withdrawal clusters by task, condition, order, language, accommodation, channel, burden, privacy, or adverse event. Never exclude a withdrawal to improve a threshold.

## Accessibility, Cultural Fit, and Subgroups

Use case-level and pattern review, not inferential subgroup comparisons in a small sample. State who was not included. No participant represents a culture, faith, sexuality/gender, disability, or recovery community. A critical harm/access failure for one person receives action even without statistical pattern.

## Researcher Reflexivity and COI

Before analysis, record founder/product expectations, facilitator/analyst roles, relevant expertise/lived experience voluntarily disclosed, financial/advocacy/fellowship interests, prior exposure to versions, and decisions made during iteration. Independent review should challenge favorable interpretation.

## Reporting

Every report states: protocol/version; authorization/reviews; recruitment/sample; materials; deviations; participant flow; all denominators; missingness/withdrawal; accommodations; adverse events; numeric distributions; negative cases; comparator/order limits; data limitations; claim ceiling; and next decision.

Do not publish raw notes, sensitive quotes, explicit details, small identifiable cells, participant stories, diagnostic labels, or efficacy/impact claims.

## Falsification and Exit

Recommend stop or major reframe when problem relevance is weak; no-tool/paper is safer or preferred; the five decisions add no value; privacy cannot be explained honestly; exit/accessibility cannot be reliable; or adverse themes persist. The objective is a valid decision, not progression.
