## Decision Frame

| Required question | Cycle 3 answer |
| --- | --- |
| User problem | Some users may want connection or stories, but sensitive recovery contexts create grooming, coercion, identity, misinformation, dependency, and authority risks. |
| Expected benefit | Bounded human stories or support may reduce isolation and improve preparation; no Better Life benefit is established. |
| Supporting evidence | Evidence-supported conclusion: online peer-support models are heterogeneous and expose safety/moderation risks. Hypothesis: Better Life-specific incremental benefit exists. |
| Required data | Prefer none for private/static use. Any story or social research needs role, consent, content, visibility, report, block, moderation, retention, withdrawal, and incident records. |
| Consent requirements | Separate choices for contributing, editing, attribution, publishing, accessibility adaptation, research, analytics, and model training. One never authorizes another. |
| Safety risks | Triggering content, shame, unsafe advice, dependency, care displacement, retraumatization, identity exposure, and moderator harm. |
| Misuse risks | Grooming, extortion, coercive partner control, sponsor/clinician impersonation, surveillance, doxxing, ban evasion, copied stories, and reward-driven disclosure. |
| Platform feasibility | Platform limitation: screenshots, external copies, re-identification, and all harmful communication cannot be prevented. Human moderation and funded operations are unproven. |
| Success metric | Before any live test: 100% role-comprehension on authority red lines in the study sample; zero Critical simulation failures; all withdrawal/incident objectives met in independent rehearsal. Thresholds require external approval. |
| Exit strategy | Disable new social access, preserve block/report/delete, remove controlled story copies, revoke roles, communicate limits, and route users to private/external alternatives. |

## Sponsor and Fellowship Boundary

- Better Life does not recruit, assign, certify, simulate, score, replace, or monitor sponsors.
- A user may privately note that they have an external sponsor; Better Life does not verify identity or share data by default.
- Any official sponsor guidance shown or linked requires Thread 03 rights, permission, attribution, and non-affiliation review.
- Any direct organization integration requires written authorization, community governance, data separation, withdrawal, and no transfer of device, protection, recovery, moderation, clinical, or emergency authority.
- `Guide`, `ally`, `peer`, `moderator`, and `professional` must not be used as euphemisms for sponsor authority.

## Story Rights and Safety Lifecycle

No story collection or publication is authorized. A future protocol must pass every stage:

| Stage | Required control | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | Name education/research purpose and why a story is necessary. | Use static/original content when a real story is unnecessary. |
| Intake | Adult eligibility, voluntary contribution, no effect on access/reward/care, no pressured trauma or explicit details. | Coercion, acute distress, unclear capacity, unsafe setting, or third-party exposure. |
| Rights | Contributor authorship/permission, third-party rights, exact media/use/geography/duration, attribution/pseudonymity. | Rights or authority unclear. |
| Consent separation | Contribution, editing, publication, research, analytics, accessibility adaptation, translation, promotion, and model training are separate. | Bundled consent or essential-service conditioning. |
| Safety edit | Contributor-controlled redaction; trained review for identifiers, third parties, illegal/unsafe instructions, triggering detail, claims, and unsafe locations. | Safety requires changing the contributor's meaning or identity protection is inadequate. |
| Preview | Exact final form, placement, audience, labels, expiry, and downstream-copy limits shown before publish confirmation. | Contributor does not confirm the exact release. |
| Publication | Source/consent receipt, non-representative label, report route, no outcome endorsement, no targeted vulnerable-user advertising. | Rights or safety state changes. |
| Withdrawal | One-step request, prompt removal from controlled surfaces, partner propagation request, retained legal record separated. | Never promise deletion from screenshots, caches, recipients, or lawful external archives. |
| Review/expiry | Periodic safety/rights check and contributor renewal where required. | Expiry, failed contact where renewal is required, changed risk, or withdrawal. |

Repository decision: stories are excluded from model training by default. Consent to publish is not consent to research, analytics, advertising, public-health reporting, or training.

## Community and Contribution Safety

- No public recovery score, streak, rank, badge tied to abstinence, disclosure, moderation labor, or peer approval.
- No reward for intimate detail, stronger surveillance, sponsor access, recruitment, or keeping another person safe.
- No unpaid requirement to moderate, rescue, remain available, or disclose relapse/setback as proof of contribution.
- No searchable identity, follower graph, unsolicited direct message, location sharing, contact exchange, or cross-context handle in an initial research concept.
- No AI-only moderation or claim that automation makes the service safe.
- No minors, international matching, or live stranger exchange without separate country, child-safety, legal, privacy, staffing, and evidence gates.

## Abuse Simulation Protocol

These are proposed tabletop/adversarial scenarios, not executed tests.

| Scenario | Input/attacker goal | Expected control | Evidence to capture | Critical failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Renamed Level 3 | Ally asks to approve a protection change through a “support check.” | Capability denied; user-controlled process continues; event surfaced. | Decision trace, copy, recovery path. | Ally can delay, deny, or learn protected history. |
| Recovery-share laundering | Feature stores a “backup token” with peer/ally/sponsor. | Schema/architecture review rejects non-user custody. | Data-flow and key-custody map. | Social actor can recover/control account. |
| Sponsor impersonation | AI/peer claims fellowship authority or personalized sponsor relationship. | Visible role source; content interrupted/reported; no sponsor label. | Transcript, moderation reason, appeal. | User reasonably believes Better Life supplied a sponsor. |
| Clinician impersonation | AI/moderator/peer offers diagnosis, therapy, psychiatry, or emergency rescue. | Stop and route to accurate scope/real-world resource. | Transcript and reviewer decision. | Clinical/emergency reliance encouraged. |
| Coerced story | Partner/employer/sponsor pressures contribution for access or reward. | Private decline, no eligibility effect, safe exit, no contact notification. | Consent path and retaliation analysis. | Story published or access changed. |
| Story re-identification | Benign text contains distinctive location, employment, or third-party details. | Contributor preview and trained redaction/hold. | Identifiers detected, human rationale. | Publication despite unresolved high re-identification risk. |

### Executed-test evidence template

Only after an approved simulation is actually run may it be labeled `Executed test result`, with: environment and version; scenario/input; expected result; observed result; timestamp; participants/roles; evidence location; deviations; pass/fail; owner; and remediation/retest decision.

## Teardown and Recovery

1. Stop new matching, messages, story submissions, and publication.
2. Preserve block, report, appeal, withdrawal, export, and delete routes for a disclosed finite period.
3. Revoke peer/ally/community tokens and verify no protection or recovery dependency exists.
4. Remove controlled story copies and notify approved distributors; disclose external-copy limits.
5. Retain only purpose-bound incident/legal evidence with owner and expiry.
6. Offer private tools and neutral external resources without claiming endorsement or continuity.
7. Publish an incident/change explanation that does not expose contributors.
