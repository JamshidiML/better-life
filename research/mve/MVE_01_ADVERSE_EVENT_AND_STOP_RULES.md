# MVE-01 Adverse Event and Stop Rules

- Version: `0.2-draft`
- Status: Draft for clinical, ethics, coercive-control, privacy, accessibility, and methods review; do not execute

## Boundary

This is a research safety plan, not clinical triage, diagnosis, treatment, crisis assessment, emergency response, or a substitute for an institution's approved policy. Reviewer-approved local procedures and current support routes are required before participant activity.

## Adverse Event Domains

- Distress, panic-like response, dissociation-like experience reported by participant, or marked discomfort.
- Shame, moral injury concern, reactance, anger, humiliation, or identity pressure.
- Rumination, compulsive repetition/reassurance seeking, or behavior worsening concern.
- Care delay, belief that Better Life replaces treatment/support, or pressure to avoid care.
- Coercion, unsafe household discovery, third-party pressure, retaliation concern, or nonvoluntary participation.
- Privacy regret, unexpected disclosure, recording/contact exposure, third-party data, or inability to delete/withdraw as understood.
- Inaccessible consent, stop, exit, discard, deletion, or other-support route.
- Explicit/illegal/third-party detail recorded despite minimization controls.
- Facilitator boundary breach, including diagnosis, counseling, persuasion, sponsor/peer role, emergency promise, or inferred calmness/capacity.
- Any unanticipated physical, psychological, social, legal, financial, accessibility, or privacy harm plausibly related to study procedures.

## Draft Severity and Response

| Level | Working description | Immediate response | Study effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S0 observation | Confusion/annoyance resolved without material distress; no critical misunderstanding remains | Offer clarification/skip; record task issue | Continue only if participant chooses |
| S1 mild | Temporary discomfort, burden, shame/reactance/privacy concern; participant wants to continue after pause | Stop current task, offer end/withdraw, apply approved control, minimal record | Safety owner reviews before next session if pattern/repeat |
| S2 significant | Material distress, coercion/privacy/accessibility concern, care-replacement belief, critical boundary failure, or facilitator breach | End session; do not probe; follow approved resource/privacy/safety process | Pause enrollment and related procedure pending independent review |
| S3 severe/critical | Serious harm, unsafe exposure, inability to leave, severe product-attributable event, emergency/clinical reliance caused by study, or uncontrolled sensitive-data disclosure | End activity; follow institution-approved emergency/incident procedure within role; preserve minimum evidence | Stop entire study; no restart without ethics/safety/founder authorization |

Qualified reviewers must approve definitions, attribution, reporting timelines, and institutional/jurisdictional duties. Researchers do not decide clinical severity beyond the approved operational procedure.

## Immediate Participant Control

At any concern:

1. Stop presenting material and stop recording/screen share where safe.
2. State that continuing is optional and no explanation is required.
3. Ask only what is necessary to apply the participant's chosen stop/privacy/support option; do not elicit a narrative.
4. Do not contact a partner, ally, family member, employer, fellowship, or professional automatically.
5. Use only approved current support/emergency routes and explain Better Life/researcher limits.
6. Explain what prototype material and research records exist and the approved withdrawal/deletion options.
7. Record the minimum incident facts separately with restricted access.

## Individual Stop Rules

End the participant's session when any of these occurs:

- Participant says stop/withdraw or behavior indicates they cannot continue under the approved consent procedure.
- Critical scope/storage/exit misunderstanding persists after one approved clarification.
- Participant cannot independently access stop/exit with required accommodation.
- Participation appears coerced or the current environment becomes unsafe/private control is lost.
- Material distress, shame, reactance, rumination, privacy regret, or care-displacement concern occurs.
- Facilitator crosses the nonclinical role or unnecessary explicit/third-party detail is being collected.
- Recording/data control fails or the approved protocol/material version cannot be confirmed.

The participant may end for any other reason. No adverse-event threshold is required for withdrawal.

## Study Pause Rules

Pause new sessions immediately for:

- Any S2/S3 event.
- Any inability to exit/discard or critical privacy/consent failure.
- Any coercive enrollment, unauthorized contact/recording/data disclosure, or unsafe discovery event.
- Repeated S1 events with a common feature, wording, population, facilitator, language, or accommodation.
- A reviewer/ethics/counsel direction, expired approval, protocol mismatch, or missing current support route.
- Any material amendment made without required approval/version control.

## Study Stop Rules

Stop rather than revise within the current study when:

- A severe attributable harm or uncontrolled privacy breach occurs.
- The concept systematically implies diagnosis, morality, treatment, surveillance, or stronger control.
- Safe voluntary participation cannot be separated from coercive/unsafe contexts.
- Critical accessibility or exit cannot be made reliable in the intended modality.
- Benefit is weak while harm/burden persists across an approved formative round.
- The required qualified safety, ethics, privacy, or clinical oversight cannot be maintained.

## Minimal Incident Record

Record only: coded participant ID; date/time band; protocol/material version; task/condition; event domain and operational level; participant-chosen action; immediate control; researcher role; data exposure class; whether study paused; report recipients; unresolved action/owner/deadline. Exclude explicit narrative, diagnosis, unnecessary quotation, exact location, employer, third-party identity, and browsing/content details.

## Review and Restart

Restart requires documented disposition by the appropriate independent reviewers, ethics body where applicable, privacy/security owner where data is involved, accessibility/coercive-control specialist where relevant, and founder. The response must identify root cause, affected versions/participants, amendment, re-consent/follow-up decision, residual risk, and whether prior results remain interpretable.

No aggregate score, user demand, founder preference, or document quality score can override an individual Critical/High safety gate.

## Follow-Up

No default follow-up. Any safety follow-up must be approved, proportionate, separately disclosed/consented where applicable, discreet, and performed by a competent role. It must not create a care relationship or imply ongoing monitoring.
