## Known Risks

- Partner/family/employer pressures participation, goal choice, disclosure, or plan retention.
- Shared devices, calendars, email/SMS, compensation, recordings, printouts, or browser artifacts reveal participation.
- Researcher asks safety-screen questions that increase danger or creates an unsupported rescue expectation.
- Withdrawal or deletion creates visible traces or retaliation.
- `Calm`, `protection`, `recovery`, or `high risk` language is weaponized.
- Future role labels recreate veto or key custody.

## Prohibited Assumptions

- Voluntary click/consent proves freedom from coercion.
- Accountless/local/no ally means safe in a shared household.
- The researcher can assess danger, provide emergency response, or safely contact another person.
- A trusted person is safe, available, or non-coercive.
- Stronger control is beneficial for a person reporting coercion.
