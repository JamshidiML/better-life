## Decision Frame

| Required question | Cycle 3 answer |
| --- | --- |
| User problem | People may need simple immediate guidance and, over time, optional learning, protection, connection, evidence, and review without being trapped in a clinical, ideological, surveillant, or engagement-driven system. |
| Expected benefit | A small Supportive experiment may test immediate usefulness; a governed long-term map preserves expansion ideas without treating them as current scope. |
| Supporting evidence | Evidence-supported conclusion: bounded mechanisms merit research. No Better Life product, pathway, mode, reward, learning system, community, or long-term outcome is validated. |
| Required data | First MVE: five local decisions or no-save session. Future views inherit canonical purpose, consent, data, rights, recovery, and teardown contracts. |
| Consent requirements | Every optional capability/purpose is separate. Setup is user-initiated with voluntary confirmation/comprehension; no mental state or capacity inference. |
| Safety risks | Shame, burden, rumination, lockout, care delay, false confidence, coercion, exclusion, manipulative learning/rewards, and clinical impersonation. |
| Misuse risks | Surveillance, partner control, diagnosis advertising, hidden influence, sponsor/peer authority, vulnerable-user targeting, and evidence overclaim. |
| Platform feasibility | Low-fidelity and local Supportive research is feasible to prepare. Every enforcement, cloud, AI, social, reward, and learning layer remains unproven. |
| Success metric | Frozen MVE comprehension, usefulness, burden, shame/reactance, privacy-trust, exit, and adverse-effect gates; future capabilities add their own gates. |
| Exit strategy | One-step close/no-save/delete for MVE; modular disable/reset/export/teardown for future layers; direct care/safety routes never depend on completion. |

## Living Recovery Loop

```text
Sense -> Understand -> Guide -> Protect -> Connect -> Learn -> Review -> Improve
```

| Loop step | Long-term meaning | Red line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sense | User explicitly provides or separately consents to a necessary signal. | No hidden monitoring or inference of religion, sexuality, diagnosis, trauma, criminality, moral worth, incapacity, or calmness. |
| Understand | System/user organize context and uncertainty in visible language. | No diagnosis, risk truth, or fabricated certainty. |
| Guide | Offer optional reviewed action, explanation, or route. | No psychiatrist/psychologist/therapist/sponsor/emergency/human-peer impersonation. |
| Protect | Apply user-authored bounded control where approved. | No universal/impossible-bypass claim, social authority, inaccessible recovery, or silent change. |
| Connect | Route to chosen real people/resources under visible roles and consent. | No live network without human safety operations; no peer/sponsor enforcement or emergency duty. |
| Learn | User learns transparently; system adapts locally from explicit choices where approved. | No covert persuasion, compulsory course, hidden profiling, or automatic global-model training. |
| Review | User sees plan, evidence, permissions, outcomes, harms, expiry, and choices. | No score, shame, forced renewal, or care barrier. |
| Improve | Human-governed evidence/content/product updates with rollback and explanation. | No automatic paper-to-guidance promotion or silent protection/personal-plan change. |

## Product Horizons

| Horizon | Candidate experience | Status and gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H0: first MVE | Four low-fidelity views for five local decisions and one optional Supportive action. | Protocol review only; no implementation or participants yet. |

| H2: synthetic adapter research | Disposable browser/OS/network spikes using synthetic domains/accounts. | Separate technical protocol; no participant enforcement. |
| H3: bounded Friction research | One approved adapter/context and exact recovery/health/teardown UX. | Supportive incremental value plus adapter/privacy/security/accessibility/coercion gates. |
| H4: Strict research | Exact finite protection and independent recovery rehearsal. | Not offered until Friction benefit and all external gates; no social co-approval/recovery custody. |
| H5: optional pathway/learning | Original or authorized stages, explicit-choice variants, inspect/reset/delete learning. | Rights, clinical, cultural, accessibility, privacy, and pathway evidence. |
| H6: connection/stories | External routes, rights-managed stories, then only if justified bounded social support. | Community/safeguarding/moderation/rights/staffing gates; not first MVE. |
| H7: rewards/partners/impact | Private acknowledgements or benefits and governed impact reporting. | Reward/economic/legal/equity/fraud/partner-data evidence; no essential-support dependency. |

Failure at one horizon does not justify adding complexity. Each module has its own removal path; the useful local core must not depend on future layers.

## Long-Term Information Architecture

| View | User question | Candidate content | Excluded pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Today | What would help now? | One optional action, close, care/resource route, no-save. | Feed, fear alert, streak, upsell. |
| My plan | What did I choose? | Five-decision plan; later optional stage/path, version, review/expiry. | Diagnosis, confession, partner-authored goal. |
| Guide | Why this suggestion? | Source, purpose, explicit preference/rule, alternatives, uncertainty. | Human/clinician/sponsor impersonation. |
| Protect | What is active and where? | Exact mode/layer/scope/health/gaps/pending/expiry/recovery. | Composite fully-protected score. |
| Learn | What can I learn now? | Transparent optional microlearning, depth, format, no-save, source/evidence. | Hidden lesson, compulsory sequence, manipulative reward. |
| Connect | Who is this and what can they do? | External resource/professional/community/chosen person with role/visibility/exit. | Implied endorsement, emergency promise, device authority. |
| Evidence | How current and certain is this? | Classification, source, population, version/date, certainty, applicability, conflict, change reason. | `Latest valid treatment`, auto-authority, freshness-as-certainty. |
| Progress/review | Is this useful or harmful? | Agency, fit, burden, shame/reactance, repair, optional user outcomes, missingness. | Recovery/risk score, abstinence rank, DAU proxy. |
| Rewards | What is being recognized and why? | Optional private acknowledgement, eligibility/data/partner/expiry/stop. | Leaderboard, streak loss, randomness, intimate proof. |
| Privacy/control | What is stored/shared/learned? | Six purposes, receipts, permissions, inspect/reset/delete/no-training/export. | Bundled consent or hidden processor/model use. |
| Recovery/teardown | How do I regain access or leave? | Immediate safety recovery, inventory, residuals, partial result, uninstall/replacement. | Approval dependency, intimate explanation, false delete-all. |

## Transparent Optional Microlearning

Every learning item shows purpose, estimated time, source/evidence state, why it appears, saved/not-saved state, and `skip`, `less`, `more`, `not relevant`, or `stop learning`.

Candidate forms: short story with rights, scenario, reflective question, visual explanation, voice/text, simulation, adaptive summary, spaced retrieval, just-in-time explanation, and culturally reviewed example. Games/challenges require separate reward/manipulation review.

Prohibited: subliminal persuasion, disguised education, deceptive defaults, variable-ratio rewards, emotional manipulation, artificial urgency, compulsory sequence, undisclosed behavioral influence, and optimizing time-in-product.

## Evidence Freshness UX

Evidence status contains separate fields:

- classification: Verified, Evidence-supported, Platform limitation, Hypothesis, Open question;
- source and retrieval/review date;
- population/context and Better Life transfer distance;
- certainty/quality and harms/non-benefit reporting;
- applicability/cultural/accessibility limits;
- contradiction/search-completeness state;
- content/recommendation version and human approvers;
- `why this changed`, previous version, rollback/expiry.

Freshness never implies correctness, certainty, treatment validity, universal fit, or automatic personalization. An evidence update cannot silently alter a plan, mode, protection, consent, care route, or reward.

## Reward and Personalization UX

- Rewards are absent from H0/H1 and never unlock safety, recovery, exit, care, privacy rights, or essential support.
- Private acknowledgement names the target behavior and evidence uncertainty; it is dismissible, no-save, and has no loss animation.
- Partner benefit shows sponsor, exact proof/data, no intimate-data statement, eligibility uncertainty, tax/legal note, expiry, and alternative without benefit.
- Personalization shows explicit input/rule, effect, edit/reset/delete, no-training, and static alternative.
- Country, culture, religion/secular preference, occupation/routine, household, accessibility, learning style, history, device context, care context, and support network are optional user-provided contexts only when necessary; never inferred from behavior.

## MVE Protocol Freeze v0.1

### Research question

Hypothesis: a five-decision, Supportive-only, local/no-save experience improves immediate perceived agency/usefulness over a paper/no-tool comparison without unacceptable burden, shame/reactance, privacy misunderstanding, or exit failure.

### Participant frame

- Adults 18+ who voluntarily describe a mismatch between a chosen goal and behavior/context; no diagnosis or abstinence identity required.
- Include varied gender/sexuality, faith/secular preference, culture/language, disability/accessibility, literacy, and prior/no recovery experience where ethically recruitable.
- This low-fidelity study is not appropriate as urgent, emergency, diagnostic, or treatment support. People seeking immediate clinical/emergency help receive direct reviewed routes without enrollment pressure.
- Proposed formative sample: 12-20, justified and revised by method review before enrollment; no population/effect estimate.

### Data and consent

Consent, withdrawal, recording, quotes, sensitive disclosure, research data, retention, deletion, and publication are separate. Use synthetic/participant-chosen broad labels; no explicit-content detail, browsing history, account, external AI, product analytics, model training, peer/ally send, or enforcement.

### Frozen measures and progression thresholds

These thresholds are protocol hypotheses frozen before participant work. Specialist review may amend them only before enrollment with a versioned rationale; after enrollment, amendments require ethics/method governance and separate reporting.

| Gate | Frozen threshold | Decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical harm | Zero product-attributable severe adverse event, coercive use, care delay, privacy disclosure, or inability to leave. | Any event pauses study and triggers independent review; aggregate benefit cannot override. |
| Critical comprehension | Every participant correctly states: non-clinical; no monitoring/blocking; local/no-save behavior; leave/delete choice. | Any miss requires copy/flow revision before progression and explicit subgroup analysis. |
| Exit success | 100% can close/discard and reach other-support route without account, payment, disclosure, or facilitator action. | Any failure is a Critical design stop. |
| Task completion | At least 80% complete five decisions without facilitator correction; report skips/withdrawals in denominator. | Below threshold: simplify/research problem; do not add automation. |
| Recall | At least 80% recall chosen action, fallback, and saved/no-save state after the session delay. | Below threshold: revise; no digital progression. |
| Usefulness/agency | At least 60% rate both usefulness and agency >=4/5, with no worse median than paper/no-tool comparator. | Insufficient value stops or reframes MVE; not MVP acceptance. |
| Burden | Median <=2/5 and no more than 20% >=4/5. | Exceeding threshold requires reduction and retest. |
| Shame/reactance | Median <=1/5; no participant >=4/5 attributable without immediate independent review. | Severe individual response pauses; subgroup pattern blocks progression. |
| Privacy trust/comprehension | 100% correctly identify what is stored, where, and how to use once/delete; at least 80% rate control >=4/5. | Any critical misunderstanding requires revision; no external flow added. |
| Accessibility | 100% complete critical consent/exit tasks with required accommodations; no unresolved blocker. | Block progression for affected mode/language/platform. |
| Qualitative contradiction | No unresolved theme indicating diagnosis, moral judgment, surveillance, care replacement, or stronger-mode pressure. | Resolve and retest even if numeric gates pass. |

Passing permits only consideration of an accountless local Supportive prototype. It does not accept an MVP, authorize production, or permit enforcement/AI/social/reward layers.

### Adverse-effect procedure

At each task and end: optional stop, burden/shame/reactance/distress/privacy-regret check, direct route to independent support, minimum incident record, and follow-up only under protocol. Researchers never pressure completion, interpret diagnosis, or provide therapy/emergency response.

## Stage Acceptance

- H0 progression: all frozen MVE gates plus clinical/method/privacy/accessibility/coercion/lived-experience approval.
- Adapter spike: separate synthetic test protocol and `BL-CAP-01`/`BL-PLC-01` conformance.
- Friction/Strict: adjacent-mode incremental benefit and all platform/safety/recovery/teardown gates.
- Actual MVP: independent score >=95, zero Critical/High, founder approval, completed external gates, and no unresolved implementation evidence gap.

Founder approval cannot waive safety, privacy, consent, clinical, legal, security, accessibility, or coercion gates.
