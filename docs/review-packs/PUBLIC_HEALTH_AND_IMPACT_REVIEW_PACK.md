## Known Risks

- Collapsing distinct addictive/compulsive behaviors into one category or global total.
- Association, output, engagement, or self-report presented as causal impact.
- Productivity/employer value creates worker surveillance or discrimination.
- Public reporting exposes small groups or stigmatizes cultures/regions.
- Quality-of-life or cost methods monetize dignity or double count outcomes.
- Digital access, care access, language, gender, culture, and disability inequities are hidden.

## Specific Questions

1. Are MVE-01 outcomes correctly limited to formative product research?
2. Which public-health concepts or words should be removed from participant/public materials?
3. Are burden entry/comparability gates sufficient, and who should approve aggregation?
4. What future outcome hierarchy separates process, product integrity, intermediate outcomes, health, social, and economic impact?
5. What counterfactual, follow-up, confounding, attrition, equity, and adverse-effect evidence would be required for causal claims?
6. What employer/payer/government data and control patterns must remain prohibited?
7. What reporting cell-size, geography, and stigma controls are needed before public reporting research?

## Prohibited Assumptions

- Global burden proves Better Life demand, fit, efficacy, or savings.
- Improved task completion, agency, or return intent equals recovery or health improvement.
- Different conditions/behaviors or years can be summed without validated comparability.
- Employer/payer funding permits individual activity/outcome reporting.
- Economic valuation can represent the value of a life or dignity.
