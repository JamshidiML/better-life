## Known Risks

- Collapsing distinct addictive/compulsive behaviors into one category or global total.
- Association, output, engagement, or self-report presented as causal impact.
- Productivity/employer value creates worker surveillance or discrimination.
- Public reporting exposes small groups or stigmatizes cultures/regions.

- Digital access, care access, language, gender, culture, and disability inequities are hidden.
