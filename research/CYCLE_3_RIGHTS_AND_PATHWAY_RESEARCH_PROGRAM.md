## Two Tracks, Never Blended

### Track A: Licensed or Authorized Fellowship Pathway

Track A is blocked unless all gates pass:

1. Written permission or a clearly applicable license covers the exact material, media, geography, product use, adaptation, distribution, storage, and accessibility transformations.
2. Trademark and affiliation wording is approved; authorization is not represented as endorsement unless endorsement is explicit.
3. The relevant organization or delegated community-governance body reviews the digital context, attribution, updates, and withdrawal process.
4. Users see source, version, relationship, license/permission scope, and alternative paths before choosing.
5. Clinical, safeguarding, inclusion, accessibility, privacy, and rights reviews approve the participant-facing pathway.
6. A takedown/withdrawal route, version freeze, export/deletion behavior, and migration to another path are defined.

Repository decision: no permission means no Track A. Linking to an official resource does not authorize copying or adaptation.

### Track B: Original Better Life Recovery Pathway

Track B uses original expression and user-authored choices. It may research broad mechanisms such as self-observation, hope, values, responsibility without shame, safe repair, reflection, chosen secular/spiritual practice, contribution, setback repair, and connection.

Track B must not reproduce or simulate protected Steps, Traditions, prayers, readings, chips, sponsor roles, meeting scripts, organizational identity, or a confusingly similar sequence. Its content requires a provenance ledger, independent similarity review, and legal/community sensitivity review.

Repository decision: Track B is not a fellowship, treatment, universal abstinence program, or substitute for professional care.

## Rights-Aware Review Procedure

For each source:

1. Record the exact official page/material, publisher/rights body, version, URL, and retrieval date.
2. Record the explicit license or terms; do not infer permissions from availability.
3. Classify separately: reading/access, quotation, repository storage, adaptation, redistribution, translation/accessibility transformation, commercial use, and model training.
4. Mark unclear fields `Legal review required`; the operational default is no copying, storage, adaptation, redistribution, or training.
5. Record trademark and affiliation constraints separately from copyright.
6. Set reviewer, evidence location, expiry, withdrawal/takedown route, and recheck trigger.

## Non-Affiliation and Community Governance

Every named-source context must state, in tested language:

- the source organization;
- Better Life's actual relationship, including `none` where applicable;
- whether material is linked, quoted, adapted, licensed, or original;
- who approved the use and when approval expires;
- where the official source can be found;
- how the user can choose another path;
- how the rights holder/community can report a problem or request correction/removal.

Proposed control: a community-governance panel includes authorized organizational representation where available, people with varied lived experience, secular and spiritual perspectives, cultural/inclusion review, safeguarding, accessibility, and legal/IP expertise. No single founder, fellowship representative, clinician, or model approves universal fit.

## Role and Impersonation Boundary

- Repository decision: Better Life and its AI do not impersonate a psychiatrist, psychologist, therapist, sponsor, emergency service, or human peer.
- Repository decision: an external sponsor remains part of that fellowship's own relationship; Better Life does not create or automate that role.
- Repository decision: original ally or guide roles, if researched later, must be visibly non-clinical, non-fellowship, non-emergency, voluntary, finite, and unable to control devices, recovery, exit, or care.

## Founder Lived-Experience Governance

The founder register separates `observed recurring problem`, `personal experience`, `fellowship norm`, `product hypothesis`, `safety concern`, `unmet need`, `design insight`, and `claim requiring external evidence`.

- Founder decision: entries from Issue #44 are preserved as vision/design inputs.

- Proposed control: founder confirmation is required before any entry is treated as personal lived experience.
- Repository decision: founder experience never establishes prevalence, universal need, clinical truth, safety, effectiveness, or representative fit.

## First MVE Boundary

`MVE` means `Minimum Viable Experiment`.

The first MVE is small, local, accountless, practical, Supportive-only, and non-clinical. It includes no licensed pathway, step course, enforcement, rewards, peer network, sponsor-like role, model learning, or clinical content. Pathway research may inform later protocols only after rights, safety, and user-research gates.
