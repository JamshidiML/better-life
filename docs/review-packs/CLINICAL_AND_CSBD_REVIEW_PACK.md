## Purpose

Obtain a qualified clinical/sexual-health review of nonclinical boundaries, CSBD/PPU language, participant safety, candidate measures, adverse-event handling, and care routing before any participant activity.

## Product Boundary

MVE-01 is a low-fidelity, Supportive-only research concept for adults who voluntarily describe a mismatch between a chosen goal and a behavior or context. It does not diagnose, screen for eligibility by diagnosis, treat, monitor, block, infer risk/calmness/capacity, provide emergency response, or replace professional care. It excludes Friction, Strict, AI personalization, peers, rewards, fellowship delivery, sync, payment, and production software.

## Known Risks

- Diagnosis or treatment implication from `recovery`, `plan`, `high-risk`, or CSBD/PPU language.
- Shame, moral/religious pressure, rumination, distress, compulsive repetition, or care displacement.
- Facilitator drift into assessment, therapy, reassurance, or emergency duty.
- Unprompted explicit disclosure or third-party information.
- Excluding people who need accessibility, language, or culturally safer alternatives.

## Specific Questions

1. Which target-user and exclusion wording is clinically honest without pathologizing high desire, moral incongruence, or culturally shaped goals?
2. Are the five decisions and interview prompts likely to elicit diagnosis, confession, or explicit detail?
3. Which Supportive action categories are safe enough for low-fidelity evaluation, and what cautions are required?
4. Are the adverse-event severity levels and immediate stop criteria adequate?
5. Which care/help-seeking prompts are neutral, non-assessing, and jurisdiction-safe?
6. Are shame, reactance, burden, privacy regret, rumination, distress, and care displacement sufficient harm domains?
7. What follow-up, if any, is ethically justified for this nonclinical formative study?

## Prohibited Assumptions

- A diagnosis, symptom severity, treatment need, abstinence goal, incapacity, or calmness may be inferred.
- Evidence from psychotherapy, mutual aid, addiction, or another condition proves Better Life benefit.
- A participant's disclosure creates a clinician-patient relationship or emergency-service capability.
- Passing comprehension/usability thresholds establishes clinical safety or efficacy.
