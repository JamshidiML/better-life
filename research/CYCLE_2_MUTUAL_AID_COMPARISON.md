## Method And Limits

Cycle 2 reviewed official descriptions from AA/TSF evidence, SMART Recovery, LifeRing, Recovery Dharma, Women for Sobriety, and peer-support research. The sample compares mechanisms; it is not a complete organization ranking, endorsement, or systematic effectiveness review. Alcohol and mental-health findings do not establish PPU/CSBD or Better Life outcomes.

## Generic And Model-Specific Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Generic mutual-aid form | Model-specific form | Transfer rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared experience | Voluntary first-person account | Fellowship testimony, topic share, or structured check-in | Peers label experience as personal; no universal prescription. |
| Mutual help | Listening, practical support, encouragement | Sponsor, convenor, facilitator, trained peer | Better Life does not copy sponsor authority; every role is bounded. |
| Belonging | Repeated contact and recognition | Fellowship identity, secular group identity, sangha, identity-specific group | Belonging cannot require one theology, anti-theology, diagnosis, or abstinence identity. |
| Service | Voluntary contribution | Meeting roles, outreach, group governance | Never require service as proof of progress; protect peers from unpaid safety labor. |
| Structured meeting | Agenda, time boundary, participation norms | Steps/readings/tools/meditation/acceptance statements | Do not reproduce proprietary or doctrinal structure; test original neutral preparation only. |
| Accountability | User asks another person to witness a chosen commitment | Sponsor contact, check-in, peer acknowledgement | Voluntary accountability is support, not surveillance, enforcement, or permanent veto. |
| Facilitation | Keeps time, protects norms, directs conflict process | Peer facilitator or clinician-led group | Safety-critical moderation requires trained accountable humans; peer facilitation is not clinical care. |
| Recovery identity | Optional shared language and hope | `recovering`, fellowship-specific or abstinence identity | Better Life never assigns identity; users may use their own words. |

## Model Comparison

| Model | Source-verified description | Distinctive mechanisms | Inclusion/exclusion caution | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AA/TSF | Cochrane reviewed peer-led AA and professionally delivered TSF for adults with alcohol use disorder. | Meetings, 12-step facilitation, mutual help, service, recovery identity. | Spirituality/abstinence/identity may fit some people and not others. | Alcohol-specific evidence cannot transfer to PPU, Better Life, or a digital network. |
| SMART Recovery | Official materials describe a secular 4-Point Program using motivation, urge coping, thought/behavior/problem management, and life balance. | Structured tools and facilitated mutual support. | CBT-style language, abstinence/choice framing, and facilitator quality still require fit review. | Organization description is not Better Life efficacy evidence. |
| LifeRing | Official materials describe secular peer meetings and a personal recovery program. | Personal plan, peer conversation, secular identity. | Substance-focused and abstinence framing may not fit every Better Life user. | Mechanism source only. |
| Recovery Dharma | Official materials describe a peer-led program grounded in Buddhist practices, meditation, inquiry, and community. | Meditation, ethical practice, shared service, decentralized meetings. | Buddhist grounding may welcome some and alienate others; no universal fit. | Mechanism source only. |
| Women for Sobriety | Official materials describe trained peer-led meetings and 13 Acceptance Statements for women addressing substance use. | Gender-specific support, self-efficacy, structured affirmations. | Gender eligibility and substance focus limit transfer; vendor efficacy language is not independent evidence. | Mechanism source only. |
| Clinician-facilitated group | Clinical literature describes professional responsibility, structured intervention, and safeguarding. | Assessment, treatment, crisis/care boundaries. | Cost, access, power, and regulated responsibilities differ from mutual aid. | Better Life cannot simulate clinical facilitation. |
| Digital peer support | Reviews report heterogeneous models and mixed-to-modest outcomes in mental health contexts. | Asynchronous forums, one-to-one or group support, moderation. | Triggering content, misinformation, hostility, privacy, and moderator burden. | No direct PPU/Better Life effect established. |
| Chosen ally | User selects a known person for a bounded support role. | Message, acknowledgement, time-limited check-in. | Coercion, relationship change, absence, compromise, and unequal power. | Product hypothesis requiring Thread 04/07 safety controls. |

## Harm, Exclusion, And Inclusion Evidence

| Area | Classification | Finding or gap | Better Life requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Triggering content | Evidence-supported conclusion | Peer-support reviews identify triggering material as a plausible/observed risk in sensitive settings. | Non-explicit rules, report/block, trained moderation, no live test before safety gates. |
| Misinformation/hostility | Evidence-supported conclusion | Moderator research notes unsafe communities can spread misinformation or hostile interactions. | Human accountability and content/appeal protocol. |
| Theology/anti-theology | Hypothesis | Spiritual and secular models offer different fit; neither is universal. | Values-neutral product language and neutral external options. |
| LGBTQ+, gender, culture | Open question | Cycle 2 did not establish direct representative evidence for Better Life's target population. | Diverse lived-experience review before any recommendation or network. |
| Confidentiality | Evidence-supported conclusion | Group norms cannot prevent screenshots, account compromise, or onward disclosure. | Never promise anonymity/confidentiality as a technical guarantee. |
| Dependency/power | Hypothesis | Repeated peer or ally contact may create authority or dependency. | Time limits, role boundaries, replacement, withdrawal, independent recovery. |
| Negative group experience | Open question | The current sample does not quantify Better Life-relevant adverse group experiences. | Prespecify adverse-event interviews and stop thresholds. |

## Human, AI, Peer, Ally, And Platform Responsibility

| Activity | User | AI/tool | Peer/community | Chosen ally | Better Life platform | Professional/emergency service |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goal and values | Owns choice | May present reviewed neutral prompts | May share experience | May listen | Must preserve autonomy | May support in a formal care context |
| Voluntary accountability | Defines exact request and expiry | May deliver confirmed message | May acknowledge, never enforce | May acknowledge within scope | Records consent/withdrawal; prevents secret role | Not normally involved |
| Device-control policy | Owns calm-state decision | May explain status, never secretly change | No authority | No authority by default; any future co-approval is a separate Thread 04/07 system | Accountable for controls, recovery, audit, and abuse handling | Not delegated to community |
| Sponsorship | May use external fellowship relationship | Must not impersonate sponsor | Existing fellowship owns its own roles | Better Life ally is not a sponsor | No imitation or endorsement | Not applicable |
| Moderation | Report/block/appeal | Triage only if reviewed | Cannot adjudicate own conflict | No moderation authority | Accountable trained human operation | Consulted under protocol |
| Clinical/crisis | Chooses care route | Static/routed information only | Encourage real-world help; no solo rescue | Encourage real-world help | Clear limits and localized routes | Own clinical/emergency response |
| Exit | May leave at any time | Executes request | No veto | No permanent veto | Must remove access and preserve appeal/evidence only as disclosed | Not normally involved |

Anonymous peers and recovery communities never receive device-control, recovery-key, policy-change, or teardown authority.

## Intellectual Property And Affiliation Questions

Open legal questions: names, logos, copyrighted readings, meeting scripts, trademark use, directory linking, deep links, and whether product copy implies endorsement. No legal conclusion is made. Better Life uses original vocabulary and does not reproduce Steps, Traditions, prayers, chips, sponsor roles, or organizational identity.
