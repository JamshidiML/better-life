## Method and claim boundary

- **Executed test result:** Cycle 1 sampled official product pages/app listings and authoritative organization pages; it did not perform hands-on product tests, purchase subscriptions, inspect code, or obtain vendor confirmation.
- **Design requirement:** A vendor-stated feature or benefit is marketing evidence about positioning, not clinical evidence or independent proof that the feature works safely.
- **Open question:** Pricing, regional availability, platform capabilities, data practices, and terms are volatile and require a dated refresh before any external use.
- **Executed test result:** No vendor or organization has been contacted; no endorsement, affiliation, or partnership is implied.

## Market frame

| User job | Classification | Solution family | Better Life learning question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restrict access | Design requirement | Browser/device/network blockers and OS controls | Can voluntary friction remain transparent, recoverable, and useful despite bypass? |
| Create accountability | Design requirement | Monitoring/reporting to a partner or ally | Can support exist without surveillance, shame, or third-party control? |
| Learn and plan | Design requirement | Recovery/self-help apps, education, journaling | Which mechanisms are evidence-bounded and useful without therapy claims? |
| Receive human support | Design requirement | Clinicians, mutual-aid groups, helplines, chosen allies | Should Better Life route/augment rather than build a new network? |
| Protect privacy | Design requirement | Local tools, DNS/browser configuration, digital-rights practices | Can accountless/local defaults be a primary differentiator and not just copy? |

## Comparison dimensions for future hands-on research

| Dimension | Classification | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual platform coverage/bypass | Design requirement | Current version, device matrix, synthetic-domain tests, false-block and recovery results. |
| Monitoring and recipient visibility | Design requirement | Product test, permission/data-flow review, current policy/terms, exact user disclosure. |
| Consent and exit | Design requirement | Setup/remove/recovery/coercion walkthrough; partner-role permissions. |
| Data and security | Design requirement | Privacy policy is insufficient alone: inspect SDKs/flows where lawful, retention, deletion/export, processors, incident history. |
| Clinical/outcome claims | Design requirement | Exact claim, population, intervention, comparator, outcome, independent evidence, harms, and regulatory context. |
| Price/value | Design requirement | Country, date, tax, platform, free trial, cancellation, and included service. |
| Accessibility/inclusion | Design requirement | Language, disability, gender/sexuality, values/religion, shame and moral framing tests. |

## Positioning hypotheses, not claims

| Classification | Hypothesis | Falsification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hypothesis | A local/accountless personal plan plus manual Spiral may occupy a quieter space between blocker and therapy. | Users see no incremental value over OS tools/journaling or require professional care instead. |
| Hypothesis | Anti-coercion and precise platform/privacy limits are meaningful trust differentiators. | Users do not understand/trust the distinction or implementation cannot prove it. |
| Hypothesis | Repair-oriented progress is preferable to surveillance/streak-first framing for some users. | User research shows it is confusing, invalidating, or ineffective. |
