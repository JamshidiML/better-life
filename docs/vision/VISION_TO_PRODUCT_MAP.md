# Better Life — Vision To Product Map

Status: v0.1  
Date: 2026-07-15

This map prevents two opposite failures:

- losing ambitious founder ideas;
- attempting to build the entire vision in the first release.

## Horizon 1 — Foundation And Validation

Purpose: determine the first safe, useful, technically honest product wedge.

Includes:

- user and expert research;
- scientific evidence map;
- technical feasibility;
- protection-plan concept;
- privacy and consent model;
- anonymous-support safety model;
- first product requirements;
- first prototype selection.

No production prediction, continuous location tracking, open peer network, or universal device enforcement.

## Horizon 2 — Trust-First MVP

Candidate scope, subject to validation:

- simple onboarding;
- user-authored Protection Constitution;
- high-risk times and contexts;
- guided setup of existing device controls;
- one platform-specific protection component;
- protective pause / spiral flow;
- grounding and replacement actions;
- manual help request;
- non-shaming lapse review;
- local-first event records;
- clear data controls and deletion.

## Horizon 3 — Anonymous Support Pilot

Candidate scope:

- trained, screened volunteers;
- limited availability windows;
- anonymous in-app text or audio relay;
- minimal profile information;
- disclosure warnings and redaction;
- strong reporting and moderation;
- no exchange of direct contact details;
- professional escalation paths;
- pilot restricted by region, language, or recovery domain.

## Horizon 4 — Layered Cross-Device Protection

Candidate scope:

- browser extensions;
- native mobile components;
- desktop helpers;
- optional DNS/network layer;
- policy synchronization;
- tamper cooling periods;
- travel and work modes;
- transparent audit logs.

## Horizon 5 — Personalized Recovery System

Candidate scope:

- adaptive education;
- personalized recovery plans;
- intervention matching;
- progress and capability review;
- meeting recommendations;
- clinician-exportable non-explicit summaries;
- multilingual adaptation.

## Horizon 6 — Privacy-Preserving Prediction

Candidate scope:

- on-device sequence models;
- high-risk window prediction;
- explainable risk signals;
- user correction and appeal;
- local geofencing or contextual modes;
- privacy-preserving aggregate learning;
- formal bias, false-positive, and adverse-event evaluation.

## Horizon 7 — Living Recovery Network

Long-term vision:

- 24/7 global matching;
- AI-facilitated meetings;
- sponsor-like relationships with governance;
- multiple recovery domains;
- trusted NGOs, clinicians, and research partners;
- localized care and crisis routing;
- transparent, independent scientific governance;
- low-cost or public-benefit access.

## Product Selection Rule

A capability moves toward implementation only when:

- a real user problem is validated;
- the intervention mechanism is credible;
- the required data is proportionate;
- consent is meaningful;
- coercion and abuse risks are addressed;
- technical capability is confirmed;
- operational ownership is clear;
- success and failure criteria are measurable;
- the feature can be removed or redesigned if harmful.

## Current Priority

The immediate objective is not to choose the entire final architecture. It is to identify the smallest product wedge that proves Better Life can provide meaningful protection during one real high-risk moment while remaining simple, trustworthy, and privacy-preserving.
