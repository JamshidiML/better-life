## Sampling Statement

Desk-review observation: the Cycle 2 and Cycle 3 records are a **sentinel sample**. Selection aims to expose materially different architectures, governance models, and decision questions. It does not estimate market share, quality, coverage, willingness, effectiveness, or representativeness.

Systematic expansion is required when a product, purchase, partnership, public claim, country launch, referral, or funding decision depends on coverage. That expansion must pre-register sources/queries, geography, inclusion/exclusion, deduplication, extraction, dual review, missing-data rules, and refresh cadence.

## Expanded Ecosystem Taxonomy

| Family | Research question | Mandatory distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital therapeutics | Is a regulated/clinical product indicated, evidenced, approved, accessible, and distinct from Better Life's non-clinical scope? | Regulatory clearance and trials are version/indication/country specific and do not transfer. |
| Recovery support platforms | Which planning, education, connection, care-routing, or support mechanisms exist? | Product marketing versus independent evidence; live operations and privacy. |
| Fellowship/12-step apps | What official/authorized meeting, reading, or fellowship functions exist? | Rights, authorization, trademark, affiliation, community governance, and no sponsor imitation. |
| Secular/multiple-path systems | Which non-12-step models and communities offer tools/support? | Fit varies; no universal secular or spiritual path. |
| Education/gamification | Which optional learning mechanics improve comprehension? | Separate pedagogy from manipulative engagement, streaks, randomness, and outcome claims. |
| Public-health campaigns | Which awareness/trust/action patterns are evidence-informed? | No vulnerability targeting, fear exploitation, diagnosis advertising, or intimate-data audiences. |
| Reward/contingency programs | When do incentives have direct evidence and clinical/operational governance? | Evidence is population/intervention specific; no transfer to app rewards or tokens. |
| AI/cloud/education credits | What public-benefit resources might reduce access cost? | No assumed eligibility/partnership; no intimate data or sponsor dependency. |
| Foundations/NGOs | Which organizations fund, deliver, challenge, or govern relevant work? | Listing is not availability, approval, fit, or endorsement; independence and compensation matter. |
| Medical/psychiatric collaboration | Where can qualified care, research, review, and referral connect safely? | No clinician impersonation, care substitution, hidden reporting, or unlicensed practice. |
| Government/payer/public procurement | What evidence, privacy, equity, and accountability would be required? | No avoided-cost claims without validated methods; no employer/payer surveillance. |
| Platform/regulatory environment | Which platform and consumer/digital rules shape feasibility and safety? | Rules and applicability require current counsel; documentation is not compliance. |
| First-party/free/open/DIY/no tool | Can existing simpler options solve the user problem? | Always compare before building; absence of product is a required comparator. |

## Source and Evidence Boundary

| Source type | Permitted statement | Not permitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official program/vendor page | The source currently states the named scope, offer, or rule. | Behavior, eligibility, durability, safety, efficacy, privacy, willingness, or Better Life fit. |
| Official public-health/government guidance | The authority describes the cited method/policy/evidence in scope. | Better Life effectiveness or universal transfer. |
| Regulatory record | Exact status for named product/indication/version/country/date. | Class-wide efficacy or current availability outside scope. |
| Independent study/security assessment | Finding within exact method, population, version, and date. | Current or universal product truth without replication/refresh. |
| Hands-on test | Observed result for exact build/account/region/device/scenario. | General behavior beyond the tested matrix. |
| User/stakeholder research | Consented participant experience/meaning. | Prevalence, vendor fact, or population efficacy. |

## Candidate Decision Gates

Every candidate must answer before shortlist/outreach:

1. What user/research/public-benefit problem is served, and what safer/no-partner alternative exists?
2. What evidence is independent, current, direct, and applicable; what is vendor-stated or unknown?
3. What clinical/regulatory status, rights/license, trademark/affiliation, accessibility, cultural, and country constraints apply?
4. What personal data, metadata, proof, account, recipient, processor, and retention would be required?
5. Can the relationship function with no partner access to intimate plan, behavior, mode, stage, story, setback, care, reward, or learning data?
6. Could incentives, marketing, referral, or funding create coercion, vulnerable-user targeting, conflict, lock-in, or endorsement confusion?
7. What benefit/harm metric, audit, incident route, withdrawal, continuity, and termination plan exists?

Any missing answer is `Hold`. Surveillance, hidden data access, clinical overclaim, essential-support conditioning, or inability to exit is `Reject`.

## Research Sequence

1. Independent category/taxonomy challenge and missing-perspective review.
2. Systematic source expansion only for categories linked to a real decision.
3. Rights, regulatory, privacy, safety, inclusion, accessibility, and conflict screening.
4. Founder-approved synthetic hands-on protocol for selected products; no real sensitive data.
5. Consented user/stakeholder comparison including free/open/first-party/no-tool alternatives.
6. Only then consider outreach, clearly as research and without endorsement request.

## First MVE Boundary
