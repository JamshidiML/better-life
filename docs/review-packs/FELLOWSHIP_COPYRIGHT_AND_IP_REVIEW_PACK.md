## Known Risks

- Copyright/trademark infringement, false endorsement, passing off, or license breach.
- Protected sequence, prayer, role, reading, or meeting structure reproduced under neutral labels.
- Open-license conditions misunderstood, especially noncommercial use and adaptations.
- Community legitimacy treated as equivalent to legal permission or vice versa.
- Accessibility translation or model ingestion exceeds granted rights.
- Contributor/third-party story rights are incomplete.

## Prohibited Assumptions

- Online, free, official, nonprofit, educational, or personally readable means reusable.
- Citation or a disclaimer cures infringement or affiliation confusion.
- Broad recovery mechanisms are owned by no one in every expression or sequence.
- Permission from one member, reviewer, or local group binds an organization.
- Legal clearance proves clinical safety, cultural fit, or community legitimacy.
