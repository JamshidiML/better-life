# MVE-01 Research Protocol

- Protocol ID: `BL-MVE-01`
- Version: `0.2-draft`
- Status: Draft for specialist and ethics review; not approved for participant execution
- Phase: Integration and Evidence Readiness
- Study type: Proposed formative, low-fidelity, nonclinical product research

## Authorization Boundary

This protocol does not authorize recruitment, contact, consent, pilot sessions, participant data collection, prototype implementation, clinical activity, or publication. The five decisions, wireframes, measures, thresholds, sample, and comparison are draft research instruments. They may be version-locked only after all required reviews and founder authorization.

## Decision Frame

| Required question | MVE-01 answer |
| --- | --- |
| User problem | Some adults report a self-defined mismatch between a chosen goal and a behavior/context and may want immediate practical guidance without diagnosis, monitoring, or forced control. Relevance is unverified. |
| Expected benefit | A five-decision Supportive flow and manual Protective Spiral may improve immediate perceived usefulness and agency compared with a paper/no-tool alternative. This is a hypothesis. |
| Supporting evidence | Adjacent self-management and behavior-change mechanisms justify cautious research; no evidence establishes Better Life efficacy, safety, clinical value, or comparative benefit. |
| Required data | Minimum task choices, task observations, comprehension, timing, ratings, brief qualitative feedback, accessibility/accommodation record, adverse-event disposition, and withdrawal/missingness. No explicit-content detail or browsing history. |
| Consent requirements | Separate decisions for participation, recording if any, quotes, contact/follow-up, research storage, and publication. Product/local-save choice is not research consent. |
| Safety risks | Distress, shame, reactance, rumination, disclosure pressure, privacy regret, unsafe discovery, care displacement, facilitator overreach, and inaccessible exit. |
| Misuse risks | Diagnosis or moral judgment, partner/employer pressure, inferred calmness/capacity, surveillance, stronger-mode upsell, and use of formative results as efficacy evidence. |
| Platform feasibility | Paper/static accessible materials can be prepared; no production platform or storage behavior is selected or tested. |
| Success metric | Problem relevance, five-decision comprehension, task success/time, Spiral usefulness, agency, privacy trust, burden, shame/reactance, accessibility, cultural fit, exit/deletion understanding, reuse intent, adverse effects, and comparison preference. |
| Exit strategy | Stop/skip/withdraw without reason or penalty; close/discard; separate deletion request; direct neutral support routes; retire the concept if value is weak or harm/ambiguity persists. |

## Scope

### Included

- Exactly five draft decisions: current help/goal; broad context; one reviewed Supportive action; one fallback; local/use-once/discard choice.
- One optional manual, static, reviewed Protective Spiral action.
- Low-fidelity paper or static screen equivalents.
- Comprehension, task, interpretation, preference, burden, harm, privacy, accessibility, and cultural-fit research.
- Comparison with paper and/or no tool/current coping only if methods/ethics review approves the design.

### Excluded

- Friction or Strict enforcement; blocking, delay, detection, redirection, VPN/DNS/router/browser control, or adapter.
- Account, identity verification, sync, cloud product storage, payment, price research, notification, telemetry, or monitoring.
- AI personalization, risk prediction, mental-state/calmness/capacity inference, external model use, or model training.
- Peer, ally, community, sponsor-like role, story collection, live support, or automatic contact.
- Reward, streak, badge, benefit, partner, impact, or growth test.
- Twelve-Step/fellowship content, licensed pathway, original recovery pathway, theology/anti-theology, or sponsor delivery.
- Diagnosis, screening as diagnosis, treatment, clinical advice, emergency response, or efficacy evaluation.

## Primary Research Question

For adults who voluntarily describe a self-defined goal/behavior-context mismatch, is the draft five-decision Supportive-only flow understandable, usable, relevant, and acceptably low-burden and low-harm enough to justify considering a later accountless local Supportive prototype?

## Secondary Questions

1. Is the problem frame relevant without implying diagnosis, abstinence, morality, or treatment?
2. Can participants explain all five decisions and their own choices?
3. Can they complete, skip, discard, and exit without facilitator correction?
4. Is the manual Protective Spiral action useful, agency-supporting, and preferable to current coping, no tool, or paper?
5. Do participants correctly understand local/use-once/discard and the difference between prototype state and research records?
6. What burden, shame, reactance, rumination, distress, privacy regret, or care-displacement signals occur?
7. Which accessibility, language, cultural, religious/secular, gender/sexuality, literacy, or prior-recovery interpretations change fit?
8. Would participants voluntarily use a reviewed version again, and why or why not?

## Proposed Design

Open question pending methods review: a two-round formative design with 12-20 total participants, purposively recruited for varied perspectives, using a counterbalanced within-participant comparison of MVE-01 and one approved paper/no-tool condition. An alternative is separate sequential rounds to avoid carryover. No sample size, comparator, order, or allocation is approved.

This is not powered for population effects. Counts and percentages describe only the observed sample. Iteration between rounds must be versioned; results from materially different versions are not pooled as one instrument.

## Proposed Participant Frame

- Adults 18+ who independently and voluntarily describe a personally chosen goal and a broad behavior/context mismatch.
- No diagnosis, abstinence identity, treatment history, fellowship membership, explicit detail, or clinical severity is required.
- Must be able to provide informed consent in an approved language/format with required accommodations and participate from a context judged sufficiently private by the participant.
- Not appropriate when immediate clinical/emergency help is sought, research participation appears coerced, privacy cannot be protected, or consent cannot be established. These are study-suitability boundaries, not diagnoses.

The final criteria, screening method, compensation, target languages, and recruitment channels require method, ethics, clinical, coercive-control, accessibility, inclusion, privacy, and founder approval.

## Materials

- Neutral study information and consent materials.
- Five-decision static MVE flow.
- One reviewed Supportive action and one fallback set.
- Paper/no-tool comparison only if approved.

No material is participant-ready in this version.

## Procedure Summary

1. Use only an approved recruitment and safe-contact route.
2. Confirm private voluntary participation without asking for explicit details or diagnosing.
3. Provide accessible information and separate consent choices.
4. Reconfirm stop/skip/withdraw and facilitator nonclinical limits.
5. Collect only approved minimal context and problem-relevance feedback.
6. Present conditions in the approved order; record task outcomes, time, assistance, skips, and adverse signals.
7. Run teach-back for five decisions, product boundary, storage/no-save, exit/delete, and other-support route.
8. Collect measures with `Prefer not to answer` and stop options.
9. Conduct the bounded interview without eliciting explicit detail.
10. Debrief, confirm prototype state disposal, explain research-record controls, offer neutral support routes, and process compensation independently of completion.
11. Perform only approved follow-up; otherwise no contact.

## Protocol Version and Amendment Rules

- Pre-enrollment: specialist amendments create a new draft with rationale and reviewer disposition.
- After formal approval but before first participant: freeze one protocol/material/measure version and register deviations.
- After first participant: no silent changes. Safety changes may stop the study immediately; amended versions require ethics/method authorization and separate analysis.
- Material changes include population, recruitment, compensation, contact, consent, task, copy, action, comparator, order, measure, threshold, data, recording, storage, retention, follow-up, or analysis.
